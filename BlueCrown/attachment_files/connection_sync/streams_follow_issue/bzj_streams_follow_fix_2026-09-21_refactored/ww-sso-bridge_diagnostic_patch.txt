Buzzjuice Streams SSO diagnostic integration
==============================================

This is deliberately diagnostic-only. It does not change account creation,
username reconciliation, authentication, or follow behavior.

1. Copy:
   streams/bzj-wo-account-diagnostics.php

2. Near the top of:
   streams/ww-sso-bridge.php

   after the normal WoWonder bootstrap has loaded T_USERS and the database,
   add:

   require_once __DIR__ . '/bzj-wo-account-diagnostics.php';

3. Immediately after a NEW WoWonder account has been successfully mapped:

   $wo_user_id = (int)$registration['user_id'];

   add:

   if (function_exists('bzj_wo_account_diagnostics')) {
       bzj_wo_account_diagnostics(
           $wo_user_id,
           66,
           'sso_new_registration'
       );
   }

4. Immediately after an existing WoWonder account is accepted/reconciled,
   where $wo_user_id is known, add the same call with:

   'sso_existing_account_reconciliation'

5. This records the subject account and the working account 66 in:
   buzzjuice.net/data.logs/follow/account-diagnostics-YYYY-MM-DD.log

IMPORTANT:
- Do not alter the existing Wo_RegisterUser() call merely to fix this follow
  problem.
- The current SSO bridge already passes active => 1 during registration and
  verifies the resulting row. The follow defect is primarily in the follow
  state machine and request/UI path.
- The diagnostic is intended to prove whether an affected account differs from
  66 before any account-level repair is attempted.

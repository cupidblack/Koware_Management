## 1. First outreach to a confirmed rehabilitation supervisor — recommended

Good day. I am contacting you on behalf of Hopeful Way Foundation's Oxford House presentation team.

We are introducing a peer-run, alcohol- and drug-free recovery housing opportunity for adults who may benefit from more stability after rehabilitation. Some people find it difficult to return immediately to the same environments and pressures they faced before treatment. A supportive shared home can help them continue building everyday routines and reconnect with education, work and community life.

Our currently reported residence is in **Ashiaman for men**; residence is subject to eligibility, house rules, the house-members' interview and vote, and actual available space. It is **not a detox or treatment centre**. Interested adults may request a confidential follow-up themselves, or be assisted by their supervisor **with their own informed consent**.

**Interest and referral form:** https://buzzjuice.net/hopefulway-oxfordhouse
**Foundation:** https://hopefulwayfoundation.org/
**Oxford House model:** https://www.oxfordhouse.org/

We have Hopeful Way authorisation to deliver Oxford House model presentations to interested institutions. Would a brief presentation for your staff or clients be useful? Please reply here to arrange it. Thank you for supporting recovery and reintegration.

— **Hopeful Way Foundation | Oxford House Chapter Chairperson**

## 4. Chairperson story for a 20-minute presentation — only with explicit permission

I spent approximately 13 years struggling with addiction, with several attempts at recovery and relapses. My experience changed when I spent three years living in an Oxford House. During that period I completed a diploma programme and learned to practise a steady, accountable routine. Since returning home, I have maintained approximately 10 years of sobriety. That is my personal journey, not a guarantee for anyone else. I want people finishing treatment to know that they can explore supportive housing before returning to familiar pressures.

## 12. Compact supervisor information brief

**What:** Peer-run, self-supported, alcohol- and drug-free shared housing during recovery and reintegration.  
**Who:** Adults in recovery; Ashiaman currently described as **men's accommodation**. Other interests are not places currently available.  
**Clinical service:** Not a detox/treatment facility. Clinical needs are referred to qualified providers.  
**Residency:** Share costs, household duties, respect, written rules, safeguarding and a substance-free living agreement. Actual fees and arrangements are supplied in a dated residence information sheet.  
**Admission:** Personal consent → first discussion → preliminary video conversation → in-house interview → members' decision (usual Oxford House threshold: **80% positive vote**). No automatic placement.  
**House governance:** Members democratically operate their residence.  
**Enquiries:** https://buzzjuice.net/hopefulway-oxfordhouse, once published. Reply to the verified Chairperson sender to request an institutional presentation.

**Version 1.0.0 — implementation candidate | 26 November 2026 | Africa/Accra**

**Accountable owner:** HopefulWay Oxford House Chapter Chairperson. **Status:** implementation assets supplied; production deployment and approvals outstanding. This supersedes the 23-point proposal as a working plan, not the charter or approved bylaws. Use the accompanying communications, intake script, WordPress redirect plugin, deployment guide and acceptance checklist as one set.

## 1. Objective and urgent house decision

Enable supervisors to explain a credible post-treatment option, invite voluntary referrals with applicant consent and sponsor approval where their support is involved, and help suitable adults complete the interview and resident voting process. Build relationships and record future demand without advertising unavailable housing. A referral is neither a bed reservation nor an admission decision.

Internal planning baseline supplied by the campaign: one operating men's residence in Ashaiman (Ashiaman); 2 residents, 8 maximum places, 6 nominal vacancies; lease ends 31 January 2027, continuation requires **more than six** residents by 31 December 2026. This literal threshold means 7 residents and 5 net additional residents, not 4. Some prior responses interpreted it as at least six: obtain the actual Board resolution. Confirm occupancy and capacity, not merely the number of nominal beds. If exactly 6 is accepted, 4 net additional residents are needed. If one resident departs before the deadline, add one to either requirement. The objective is sustained, appropriate occupancy, never filling beds at the expense of suitability or safety.

The Chairperson must meet residents, Treasurer, landlord and Foundation leadership by 28 November to agree a written decision: current bed availability, dates and verified contribution, lease renewal/extension or bridge funding, minimum safe operation, actual financial threshold and a contingency plan. Do not accept deposits or offer moves beyond the lease without continuity arrangements. If seven is unrealistic, negotiate bridge funding or an extension now; do not wait until December ends. If closure is likely, stop new move-ins, tell applicants privately, offer verified alternatives where available, and arrange safe transition with existing residents. No pressure or public appeals using applicant identities.

Planning example only: with 25% of consent-verified enquiries ultimately moving in, five net arrivals require 20 suitable-interest enquiries, or 24 if one departure is expected. At 40%, 13 (or 15 with one departure). These are workload scenarios, not forecasts or applicant quotas. Review actual conversion weekly, and reserve capacity only after a house decision and confirmed move-in. The deadline is 35 days from the plan date; work in parallel on institutional relationships and housing continuity.

## 2. Governance and source authority

The signed presentation approval reported by the user authorizes presentations on behalf of HopefulWay. It does not establish licensing, individual-house charter coverage, admissions authority, data processing authority, or lease continuity. Keep the signed original unchanged, with version/date and controlled storage. An institution-specific cover letter may be addressed to the verified supervisor, title and postal address, signed afresh by an authorized person. If the Foundation instead wishes to personalize the approved letter itself, the original issuer must approve and reissue/sign the personalized copy; never edit a signed document or transplant a signature. Share the signed authority with first messages to known partners where welcome; for new contacts offer the authority after permission, or send it when they request verification. Use public brochure and video only after review.

The user's understanding is that HopefulWay holds an umbrella charter covering its houses. Oxford House Inc.'s public materials describe charters issued to individual houses. These can be reconciled only by inspecting the actual Ghana agreement and obtaining written confirmation from Oxford House Inc. of the local arrangement, active house coverage and approved wording. Do not assert either individual or blanket charter coverage without that evidence. A charter is a model/governance authorization, not a Ghana regulatory licence. Ask HeFRA and the relevant Ghana authorities/counsel whether any residential, health, safeguarding, occupancy or other permits apply. Do not describe other halfway houses as illegal, inferior or unlicensed without evidence. Compare concrete features: resident vote, equal expenses, written rules, peer accountability, technical support and published research.

Oxford House is supportive sober housing, not detox or clinical treatment, staffed supervision or guaranteed sobriety. Official House Traditions specify ordinary majority rule but an 80% affirmative admission vote. Verify local authorized rules; if the house has two voting members and the standard applies, both must vote yes. A general majority is not the standard admission threshold. Do not change voting rights, abstention rules, or quorum to meet occupancy targets. Foundation staff coordinate and support, not override residents. Public studies of Oxford House in the US support its model (one randomized study, 150 participants, reported lower substance use at two years, 31.3% vs 64.8% usual care); these are not Ghana-specific outcomes or individual predictions. Ghana's Recovery Africa history documents collaboration beginning in 2009 and previous houses; historical capacity is not current availability.

2. House representatives confirm current voting rules, eligibility (adult men for the current house), actual capacity/vacancy, move-in timing, continuity/contingency, visitor and accessibility arrangements.

4. Approved current house bylaws: behaviour, chores, meetings, finances, visitors, quiet hours, sobriety, permitted prescriptions, testing, relapse/readmission, complaints and safeguarding, dispute resolution, emergency procedure, and lawful removal. Confirm referrals for clinical care, crisis and alternative accommodation.

6. Chairperson approves contact list, message, and availability claims. Release only when these checks pass. If only availability is uncertain, use approved information-only outreach without a live placement promise; no referral form until privacy approval.

## 3. Referral funnel and outreach operating model

Institution verified → relevant supervisor contacted → permission to share material → optional presentation → voluntary applicant referral/self-enquiry → consent verified → information and fees explained → video preliminary interview → house interview → independent resident vote → written offer and applicant acceptance → move-in → 7/30/60/90-day voluntary support check-ins.

Do not require presentation attendance, institutional referral, sponsor, treatment completion at the point of enquiring, smartphone or public testimony. For a person in treatment, agree appropriate discharge planning with their clinical team and do not move them in until medically ready. For self/community referrals, no rehab certificate by default; adults genuinely seeking recovery housing and able to participate under approved eligibility rules are welcome to enquire, not general student housing. An applicant without a sponsor can explore self-funding or legitimate assistance. Do not require family consent for an independent adult; for a funded referral the sponsor agrees to the referral/funding where relevant, with applicant's permission to involve them. If a sponsor decision is pending, provide information but do not count the referral as sponsor-confirmed or arrange financial commitment. Never disclose to sponsors without applicant authorization. Minors or uncertain legal decision-making capacity: stop intake and route to approved safeguarding/legal process.

### Institution pipeline

Start with 5–10 verified contacts, then 5–10 per working day where manageable; do not send indiscriminate bulk WhatsApp. Priority 1: rehab discharge planners/directors, addiction and mental health outpatient teams. Priority 2: social workers, recovery NGOs, faith recovery groups, community reintegration organisations. Priority 3: tertiary/student counselling and welfare offices, technical institutions, workplace support (adult recovery needs only). Obtain professional contact via official websites or office calls, identify the right decision-maker, ask permission to send the pack, and log source/date. No group messaging or harvested personal contact lists. For WhatsApp Business use individual messages, not bulk automation; only use a broadcast list for recipients who have opted in, with opt-out honoured. Labels: New institution, permission requested, engaged, presentation pending, active partner, nurture, closed/opt-out. Do not use read receipts as proof of consent. Local starting points to verify: HopefulWay Centre, Recovery Africa/House of St Francis, Recovery Ghana Consortium, referral desks at mental-health and addiction services, adult tertiary counselling units. Historical affiliations are not endorsements or current contacts.

Day 0: ask permission (or send short introduction to consenting/established partners); link to direct form when deployed, optional presentation by reply. Day 3: short receipt check for eligible contacts. Day 7: share one useful resource/FAQ, not same message. Day 14: one permission-based phone call or final note if appropriate. Then pause; 60–90-day re-engagement only where welcome. Responded: stop generic cadence and agree next action. Opt-out/decline: stop immediately and maintain minimal suppression record. No named applicants in institutional messages. Use the separate institution register created by script; assign owner, next due date, contact source/permission, date and aggregate outcomes.

## 4. Intake and system architecture

One public URL immediately opens the form, with compact introduction and official links in the form itself. `https://buzzjuice.net/hopefulway-oxfordhouse` is primary; `https://koware.org/hopefulway-oxfordhouse` is alternative. Both use the identical published Google responder URL with a temporary 302 redirect. This honors the requested direct loading and avoids multiple clicks or iframe failures. An additional optional resource page can be created later; it is not a gate. The included WordPress plugin configures this without hard-coded unknown Google IDs. If Buzzjuice is not WordPress at that path, use a host-approved HTTPS 302 rule. Do not redirect to the edit or response sheet URL. Both proposed public routes returned 404 during review; do not send them yet.

Google Form supports self and assisted submissions, minimal fields, explicit applicant agreement before entering any personal details, then route-specific questions. Required: adult affirmation, affirmative consent, submission route, names, preferred contact channel and safe contact method/time (an authorized supervisor may relay contact), current area, house interest dropdown, willingness to consider Ashaiman, earliest move timing category, awareness of rules, source. Optional: desired location typed in, expected discharge or move-in date, communication support without medical information; supervisor name/institution/contact only if authorized and assisted; sponsor status and permission, no sponsor contact until separately requested. Future female/male locations explicitly interest only; no co-ed. The script generates the exact fields. If sponsor funding is pending, record it; no move-in funding commitment before agreement. Form asks no full DOB, place of birth, national ID, diagnosis, medication, clinical record, emergency contact or substance history. Those may be collected later only if justified, secure and necessary under approved policy; birthplace is generally unnecessary. Do not request attachments. Full eligibility/fees/rules and permission for sharing with residents are explained before referral to house interview.

The form responses are held by Google Forms and copied via the installable trigger into one restricted applicant Sheet; no native response destination is configured to avoid a third copy. The script assigns deterministic unique IDs from response IDs, tracks stage, consent verification, sponsor status, owner, next action, timestamps and notification state. It creates a separate institution sheet and aggregate dashboard. support@buzzjuice.net receives only reference and alert; no names, phone, recovery information or sheet link. Mailbox must be authorized and monitored; it is not a repository of responses. A periodic reconciliation recovers missed triggers and retries failed alerts; no exact-once email guarantee. All files created under Foundation-controlled Google account, not personal account. Data stays out of GitHub and public pages. A backup operator must be able to administer trigger/account access under organization policy.

### Privacy and retention

Recovery-related contact is sensitive even without diagnosis. Ghana Act 843 requires attention to registration, lawful processing, special personal data, transparency, security, processor contracts and retention. Obtain privacy lead/counsel sign-off and check current requirements before collection; confirm Data Protection Commission registration and cloud transfer/hosting safeguards. Document HopefulWay as intended controller; Google and approved technical providers as processors as appropriate, with written agreements and access restrictions. Do not use personal WhatsApp groups, trackers, calendars, or email as clinical records. Disable public summaries, edits after submit, unnecessary Google account login, tracking pixels and analytics on application route. MFA, least-privilege sharing, no public links, access review monthly and immediately on departure. Applicant sheet limited to named intake staff, Chairperson as necessary and approved backup; outreach staff see institution register only; residents see minimum interview information after separate permission. Log privacy incidents, isolate access and escalate promptly under approved legal incident procedure. No recordings by default.

The proposed retention schedule is 90 days after last meaningful contact for closed/unresponsive enquiries; future-interest renewal at 180 days only with explicit agreement; move-in records transferred to separately approved residency record system and intake copies deleted on approved schedule. Withdrawal: cease contact, review lawful retention needs, delete both form and Sheet record and any copies when permitted, record minimal nonidentifying deletion audit; handle access/correction requests via Foundation public contact, after verifying identity safely. No automatic deletion without authorized review. Update approved privacy notice if retention differs from suggested periods. Do not imply consent waives legal rights. The privacy wording in the form is a proposed release candidate, not a compliance certification.

Use a distinct Amelia service on Koware.org for institutional presentations, not for the referral form. Create page `/hopefulway-oxfordhouse-presentation/` (proposed, publish/test first), 30 minutes (20 talk + 10 Q&A), on-site and virtual services if needed, 30–60 minutes travel buffer each side as realistic, pending approval, shared Chairperson/coordinator resource/calendar, capacity one booking, no overbooking, free institutional session subject to Foundation approval, timezone Africa/Accra. Ask only institution, contact, role, business phone/email, venue, format, estimated audience, topics, accessibility logistics; never names of clients. Require approval confirmation after checking presenters/travel, add calendar busy blocks for pending requests where supported, send confirmation/reminder 24h before, allow cancel/reschedule with coordinator. Until tested, reply or call the Chairperson to arrange; add booking URL to messages only after testing. Preliminary interviews are privately scheduled with applicant/supervisor using a separate restricted calendar and reference-only titles; no applicant PII or medical data in Amelia public service.

Presentation agenda: 2 min introduction and signed authority; 3 min recovery transition and triggers without blame; 4 min peer governance and evidence; 3 min authorized Ghana house facts, contribution, rules and availability; 3 min speaker's approved personal journey; 3 min referral, consent, sponsor and interviews; 2 min current and future location options/CTA; 10 min Q&A. Show the approved Ghana video or short excerpt only with permission and within time. Bring approved signed letter, one-page factsheet, verified fee/rules sheet, official model link, tested QR/URL. No applicant names or public testimony requested. Give supervisor a client-forwardable message and agreed next action; follow up in 1–2 working days.

## 6. Applicant handling and admission SOP

Acknowledge within one working day through approved safe channel; verify consent directly with applicant before disclosing or contacting anyone else, confirm sponsor status where involved, ask if safe to discuss, deduplicate quietly by confirmed identity/contact (do not auto-merge). Assign intake owner and next date. Explain confirmed costs, location and realistic continuity; send rules in accessible form and ask if they still wish to proceed. For future demand only, ask separate permission for later follow-up; if not granted, close/delete as appropriate.

Preliminary video call: target within 3–5 working days, 20–30 minutes, applicant chooses time, supervisor coordinates for clients in rehab with permission; offer phone/in-person equivalent if video unavailable. Verify adulthood, voluntary interest, readiness for sober peer living, transition date, ability to participate/share chores and expenses, accessibility, sponsor arrangement and safe discharge plan. Ask open questions: What do you hope to achieve? What support helps you maintain recovery? What is your plan for study/work, meetings and expenses? Are you comfortable with resident votes and shared duties? Do you have questions about the substance-free and screening rules? Do you need help arranging a safe move? Do not conduct diagnosis or require public disclosure; direct medical readiness matters to qualified clinical professionals with consent. Do not score people on wealth, diagnoses or institutional influence. Record minimal outcome: proceed, defer with reason/next step, future interest, declined/withdrawn, or clinical/safeguarding referral. Explain deferral and contact options. Preliminary approval is not admission.

Before house interview: applicant agrees to share minimum necessary information with members; confirm capacity and rules, coordinate with discharge planner if relevant, schedule normally a few days before planned move. Residents interview applicant, ask consistent house-fit questions, hold independent vote with verified quorum/threshold, document date, total eligible, yes count, outcome and decision basis in restricted house records. No referral bonus, staff vote override or pressure. Inform applicant privately; if accepted, provide written fees/rules, confirmed bed and move date, applicant acceptance, check funds, orientation and receipt; if declined, explain appropriate general reason, reconsideration/complaints route as available, offer alternatives without disclosure. Do not promise a bed until these steps pass. For re-entry after relapse, use approved process and fresh vote; not automatic.

Orientation: review substance-free policy, prescription medication distinction (do not require stopping prescribed treatment), duties, meetings, rent/dues, privacy, rights, house officers, visitors, safety and complaint channels, emergency contacts and agreed support plan. At 7, 30, 60 and 90 days offer voluntary check-in on adjustment, goals, education/work, peer support and practical concerns; report aggregate outcomes only. Respect residents' autonomy and no surveillance.

### Relapse, screening and emergency

Abstinence covers alcohol and non-prescribed/illicit intoxicating drugs inside and outside the house. Members can raise concerns or request testing under verified rules; trained/authorized testing arrangement, privacy, documented results, confirmation where appropriate, legitimate prescriptions and fair refusal/dispute handling. Oxford House's model requires prompt removal for resumed use, but local implementation must follow the approved charter, bylaws and Ghana law; never use force, publicly shame, confiscate medication or promise automatic police eviction for refusal. For suspected overdose, severe withdrawal or immediate medical risk call Ghana emergency/ambulance 112 and obtain clinical care; no home detox. Safely arrange discharge, accommodation/transport and clinical referral with the resident where possible; lawful authorities only where necessary and authorized. Readmission after clinical stabilization/detox where indicated is a fresh application/vote subject to rules and vacancy. Do not promise automatic reinstatement. Escalate abuse or safeguarding concerns to Foundation lead; maintain complaint route independent of house vote.

## 7. Financial sustainability and expansion

No per-referral, per-placement, monthly rent percentage or fixed-after-stay commission in pilot; even payment after 30/60 days links professional advice to occupancy. Instead offer supervisor education, shared discharge information, recognition with permission, and documented reimbursement for actual legitimate institutional work only if approved after legal/ethics and Oxford House review, independent of placement, disclosed and never from undisclosed resident dues. No preferential admission. Treasurer prepares budget from actual rent, utilities, supplies, maintenance, reserves, contributions and shortfalls. Model 2, 6, 7, 8 occupants; monthly break-even resident count = ceiling((fixed expenses + reserves)/(approved contribution minus per-resident variable expense)), if denominator positive. No new commitments without funds and resident agreement. If a sponsor wants to support a bed, offer transparent Foundation-administered assistance subject to approved policy, not influence on vote.

Record future preferred area and men's/women's house interest separately. At least six consistently willing, eligible residents is a planning trigger for a separate women's house or new location, not an opening guarantee. Conduct repeat confirmation, charter/permissions, safe property and capacity inspection, financial reserve/lease, resident leadership and safeguarding assessment. Four-bedroom 6–9 capacity is a local planning range only, not a universal occupancy standard; actual safe/legal capacity governs. No co-ed housing in current programme. A person may choose Ashaiman if appropriate and available; never pressure them to accept it. Notify demand applicants only with opt-in; no public demand counts for tiny groups.

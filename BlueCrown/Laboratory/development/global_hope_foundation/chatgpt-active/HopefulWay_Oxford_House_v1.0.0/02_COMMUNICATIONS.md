## J. Applicant communications (only on a safe agreed channel)

**Acknowledgement:** Thank you for expressing interest in HopefulWay's housing programme. A team member will contact you through the method you selected to explain next steps. Registering does not guarantee accommodation. If your contact preference changes or you wish to withdraw, please contact the Foundation.

**Consent confirmation:** Thank you for your enquiry. Before we continue, is this a safe time and channel to discuss the information you requested? Are you personally interested in learning more, and may we contact you about next steps? If a supervisor or sponsor is involved, please tell us whether you wish us to communicate with them.

**Preliminary interview:** Thank you for confirming your interest. We would like to arrange a private introductory discussion about your goals, house conditions, contributions and next steps. Please let us know a suitable time and whether video, telephone or another accessible format works best. This conversation is not an admission decision.

**House interview invitation:** Following our introductory discussion, we would like to arrange a meeting with the residents, subject to confirmed availability and your permission to share the necessary information. The members will make their own decision under the house rules. We will explain the confirmed arrangements and costs before you decide whether to proceed.

**Accepted — preliminary notification only:** The house has voted to offer you a place, subject to the confirmed written arrangements and your acceptance. We will discuss the move-in date, costs, residence conditions and orientation privately. Please do not make payments or travel until these details are confirmed through the approved process.

**Deferred:** Thank you for taking time to discuss your interest. We are not able to proceed to a move-in at this time. We would be happy to explain the next steps privately, including any appropriate alternatives or later opportunities, if you wish.

**Future interest:** Thank you for indicating your preferred location. We do not currently promise accommodation there. With your permission, we can contact you if a suitable option is developed. You can withdraw that permission at any time.

**Withdrawal:** Thank you for letting us know. We will stop contacting you about this enquiry and handle the information you supplied in accordance with the Foundation's approved privacy policy.

## K. One-page factsheet text (public, after release approval)

**HopefulWay Oxford House — Recovery, responsibility and reintegration**

Oxford House is a peer-led, self-supporting recovery residence. Members share expenses, household responsibilities and governance. It provides an opportunity to pursue education, employment, recovery support and independent living in a sober community. It is not a clinical treatment or detoxification facility. Recovery Africa documents HopefulWay's collaboration in establishing Oxford House in Ghana in 2009. Official model information: https://www.oxfordhouse.org/ . Local Foundation: https://hopefulwayfoundation.org/ .

**Who may enquire?** Adults committed to recovery and willing to follow the house's rules, including people completing rehabilitation or referred from community, educational or family sources. The current focus is the separate men's residence in Ashaiman. Other locations and separate women's accommodation depend on demand, approval and availability; no co-ed residence is offered.

**What is expected?** Alcohol- and drug-free living, respect for others, shared chores, meetings and approved financial contributions. The house explains its written testing, relapse, emergency and complaints procedures before admission. Prescribed medication must be addressed under the approved policy with appropriate medical advice.

**How to enquire:** Voluntary registration → private preliminary discussion → house interview → resident vote → written offer and move-in if accepted. No guarantee of admission or accommodation. Sponsor funding is agreed when applicable, with the adult's consent. Confirm fees and availability with the Chairperson before committing.

Register: https://buzzjuice.net/hopefulway-oxfordhouse . Presentation requests: reply to the Chairperson. Ghana introductory video: https://youtu.be/OH2X5Ib8eIc .

## L. Presentation speaker guide (20 min plus 10 min questions)

1. 0–2 min — introduce HopefulWay, delegation and verified signed authority. Ask attendees what makes the transition after treatment difficult; do not ask for disclosures.
2. 2–5 min — explain returning to triggers, isolation and practical challenges, without blame. Supportive housing can be one option.
3. 5–9 min — explain peer governance, equal expenses, abstinence and resident admission vote. Describe evidence from published studies as US findings, not local promises. Only use precise approved Ghana charter wording.
4. 9–12 min — present current verified Ashaiman information and approved cost/rules sheet. Future houses interest-only. Explain that no one is compelled to choose it.
5. 12–15 min — approved testimony and brief Ghana video excerpt if time/permission allow.
6. 15–18 min — walk through consent, sponsor involvement, direct form, preliminary and house interview, vote and move-in.
7. 18–20 min — share tested form QR/URL and reply contact. Invite the supervisor to discuss voluntary referral privately.
8. 20–30 min — questions; record operational questions, not names or medical information. Follow up with verified answers.

**FAQ:** Is this rehab? No. Do I need rehab first? Not necessarily; readiness and rules apply. Do I have to move immediately? No, wait until clinically ready and accepted. Is it free? Costs are the confirmed house contribution and applicable fees, explained before commitment. Can I choose a location? Yes, indicate preferences; only verified existing places are available. Can women apply? Express future interest only unless a separate house opens. Who decides? Residents, under approved voting rules. What happens after relapse? Safety/clinical care and approved removal/readmission policy apply, not an automatic return. How long may I stay? Oxford House model has no fixed time limit for members in good standing; local lease and approved conditions govern actual availability. Can sponsor decide? An adult makes their own decision; funding consent is separate. How is privacy protected? Restricted intake, no group sharing; ask Foundation for access/correction/withdrawal.

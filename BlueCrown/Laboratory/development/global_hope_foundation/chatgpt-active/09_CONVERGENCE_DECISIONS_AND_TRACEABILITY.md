## A. Resolution hierarchy
1. **Documented lawful and safety requirements**, applicable house charter/bylaws and the interested adult's voluntary consent take precedence over growth targets, sponsor preferences or referring staff instructions.
2. **Current confirmed house facts** take precedence over published older examples or prior-platform extrapolations. The two residents, eight places, six-person goal and lease date are *reported*, not verified by the system.
3. **House members**, not marketing staff, decide admission in accordance with actual governing rules. Official Oxford House FAQ/traditions specify 80% favorable membership vote for new admissions, while ordinary matters use majority voting.
4. **No fabricated identities, costs, signed letters, booking URLs, Google form IDs, charter assertions, success statistics or availability claims.** If facts cannot be independently established, omit or label internally pending verification.
5. **Conversion means actual voluntary, house-approved move-ins**; this is distinct from interest, leads, introductions, application submissions and interview bookings.

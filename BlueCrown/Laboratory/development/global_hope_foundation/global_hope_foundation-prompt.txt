Our next step is to reach out to the various institutions, including rehabs and educational bodies inviting any interested individuals to apply for residence at a Hopefulway Oxford House. For clients at a rehab, this would be after their treatment. Any other interested individual coming in from any other source should be made aware of the residence rules and conditions.

A proposed action plan would be:

<<START PROPOSED ACTION PLAN>>

1. Send a broadcast WhatsApp message to rehabilitation facilities in Accra and surrounding areas.

2. The message should be brief but contain enough relevant details for supervisors at rehabs to follow up on an share with their clients. Especially details like, 'Many clients tend to relapse after their treatment by returning to the same environment. Going through the Oxford House after a client's treatment can help to ground them and reintegrate them into society before retuning to their home. I myself was in addiction for about 13 years with several recovery attempts and relapses. I stayed at an Oxford House for 3 years and managed to complete a diploma program during that time. After completing the diploma program and moving back home I have been able to maintain a steady 10 years of sobriety.' This kind of message could be shared in a presentations, maybe a shorter version if feasible to be shared in the message. 

3. The message should contain relevant links and attachments to Hopefulway and Oxford House resources on their websites:

https://hopefulwayfoundation.org/

https://www.oxfordhouse.org/

4. The message should include a link to a Hopefulway Oxford House interview waiting list where supervisors can add interested clients to the waiting list with the consent of the client's sponsors.

5. The waiting list could be implemented using google forms and would include fields like:

First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Place of Birth, Supervisor, Contact, Oxford House Location, Preferred location, Date of Discharge, {any other feasible fields}

6. A house might not be available in the client's desired location but as more people might request for that location, a house could be organized and opened in that location, so a client might have to work with the available houses till one can be opened in their desired location. The Oxford House Location could be dropdown displaying a list of existing oxford houses. A client should be able to type in their preferred location.

7. The form would be accessible through the Buzzjuice Network via:

https://buzzjuice.net/hopefulway-oxfordhouse

or

https://koware.org/hopefulway-oxfordhouse

Visiting that link should immediately load the form. Responses would be sent to support@buzzjuice.net

8. Supervisors should be able to respond to book a live presentation if they deem it necessary where the mentioned team would visit the rehab to perform a Hopefulway Oxford House presentation. A booking system would be setup for supervisors to book presentation requests. This would hel to avoid clashing dates. Amelia booking system is available on Koware.org.

9. Any interested persons on the waiting list would have a preliminary interview conducted via video call by any of the team members. The video call would be appropriately scheduled by the client's supervisor.

10. The messaging strategy should implement effective and efficient nurturing.

11. The message should let supervisors, clients and sponsors aware of the background of the Oxford House and that the Hopefulway Foundation has an official charter with the Oxford House, providing a tested foundation for a halfway house with a number of testimonials that can be viewed online. The benefits and advantages of the chartered Oxford House over an unlicensed and uncharted Halfway House. Clients, supervisors and sponsors should also be made aware of the reintegration aspect of the Oxford House where the residents help and support eachother to reintegrate into society whilst staying sober with additional support from the Hopefulway Foundation.

12. If a supervisor or institution does not respond after 3 day, a follow up message can e shared, if still no response after one ween, maybe another message or reminder could be sent or phone call could be made, if no response after two weeks, a message with a phone call could be the last resort. An attempt could be made again after a a few months.

13. The Oxford House Letter for Presentations from Hopefulway would be updated by placing the target institution's details such as address and the name of the person in charge or the supervisor's name in the block at the top left of the letter so that the letter would read as 'Dear {suprvisor_name}....

14. The Oxford House Letter for Presentations from Hopefulway would either be shared in the first message to the institution or on request. If in the first message, the letter would cover some details that would not need to be repeated in the WhatsApp message.

15. After passing the preliminary interview, a client would be invited for the in-house interview, usually a few days before the client officially moves in after passing the in-house interview. All residents in the house submit their vote to the final decision to pass a potential resident.

16. Considered is that supervisors and referrers could earn a monthly referral commission off their client's rent at a reasonable percentage, fixed rate or percentage + fixed rate.

17. There is currently only one Hopefulway Oxford House active and is located in Ashiaman with male residents. The house can accommodate up to 8 residents but there are currently only two residents in the house. That house's rental lease expires at the end of January. Hopefulway plans to close down the house if there are not more than 6 residents in the house by the end of December.

18. Female accommodation can be made available depending on demand of at least 6 residents consistently maintained in a house. Coed Hopefulway Oxford Houses are currently not supported.

19. House capacity is usually between 6 and 9 in a four bedroom house. More rooms allow a higher capacity to be maintained.

20. Anyone can stay at an Oxford House and not necessarily from a rehab. As long as the person abides by the rules to not use any narcotics or alcohol in or outside the residence. A test can be requested at any time by any resident. Failing a test or refusing to take a test would result in immediate eviction with support from the local authorities if necessary.

21. In case of a relapse, a resident can undergo immediate detox and return to the house if voted in.  

22. The house is democratically run by majority votes and residence rules that cover behavior, duties, requirements, dues, fees, rent, emergency procedures, safeguarding procedures and other due bylaws that must be adhered to.

23. The primary contact for this campaign should be the Chairperson.

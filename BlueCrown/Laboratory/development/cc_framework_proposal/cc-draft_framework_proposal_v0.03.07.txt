1. The proposed structure of the Classies Chronicles is generally:
- 1 Season per year
- 5 chapters per year (mid January to mid March, mid March to mid May, June and July, mid August to mid October, mid October to mid December)
- 8 weeks per chapter
- 2 weeks per episode
- 1 day per chronicle

1.i. Although each episode spans 10 working days, only one chronicle should be produced for one day. All ten chronicles would be eventually compiled manually and edited to produce one Classies Chronicles episode.

1.iii. An episode always begins on a Monday and ends on the following week's Friday.

1.iv. The title of an episode is determined after the last chronicle is produced for that episode. The title of a chapter is determined after the last episode is produced for that chapter. The title of a season is determined after the last chapter is produced for that season.

1.v. Instead of referencing a chronicle as 'Day 1, Day 2' and so on, use 'Chronicle #1, Chronicle #2....' and so on up to 'Chronicle #10' making one episode.

This can help to identify the first Monday of the chapter when the first episode of a chapter begins. The endpoint must be used to identify the current day of the episode.

5. Each generated chronicle might need to review previous chronicle, episode, chapter, season or other continuity ledger for consistency, relevance and reference in the produced daily Classies chronicles; so a chronicle is built in the following way:

5.i. A season 'gist' or manifest is created at the beginning of every season. All content created during the season is either based on or references the gist of the season. If a 'gist' or manifest is not available, fallback to the archived seasons.

5.ii. A chapter 'gist' or manifest is created at the beginning of every chapter. All content created during the chapter is either based on or references the gist of the chapter and season. If a 'gist' or manifest is not available, fallback to the archived chapters.

5.iii. An episode 'gist' or manifest is created at the beginning of every episode. All content created during the episode is either based on or references the 'gist' or manifest of the episode, chapter and season. If a 'gist' or manifest is not available, fallback to the archived episodes.

5.iv. A chronicle 'gist' or manifest is created at the beginning of every chronicle or acquired from the 'Daily Diary' archive. All content created during the chronicle is either based on or references the 'gist' or manifest of the chronicle, episode, chapter and season. If a 'gist' or manifest is not available, fallback to the archived chronicles.

Maintain a running visual continuity record, updated after every approved page: character positions, appearance and wardrobe; location layout; props and objects; light direction; time of day; weather; camera position; unresolved story threads. Use previously approved pages as references for the next page. A new panel must look like the same physical place unless the script marks a transition.

=====================================================================
11. STAGE 7: QUALITY ASSURANCE AND VISUAL REGRESSION TEST
=====================================================================
Before presenting any page, compare it side by side with at least three approved reference images, choosing ones closest in content (similar scene type, lighting or characters), and with the previous approved page.

Score each criterion as Pass, Minor issue or Critical failure.
A. Series identity: does it look like the same comic as the references?
B. Locked-style fidelity: linework, colour, shading, lighting, rendering.
C. Character canon: faces, skin tone, hair, proportions, wardrobe.
D. Composition and reading order: balanced, readable, correct panel flow.
E. Cinematography: intentional angles, lighting, perspective.
F. Narrative accuracy: matches the script beat by beat.
G. Lettering: exact text, legible, correct attribution.
H. Continuity: agrees with the ledger and previous pages.
I. Technical quality: sharp, coherent, free of artifacts, correct dimensions.
J. Archive readiness: suitable for the official archive without further correction.

Rules:
- Any Critical failure in A, C, F, G or H blocks approval.
- Any Critical failure elsewhere, or more than two Minor issues, requires correction.
- Correct and regenerate up to three times per page. If the page still fails, present the best attempt, list each remaining defect honestly, and ask the operator for direction.
- Never approve a page because it merely looks attractive.
- Report the scorecard to the operator in a short table with one line of evidence per failed or minor item.

=====================================================================
12. IMPROVEMENT CLAUSE: BETTER, NOT DIFFERENT
=====================================================================
Treat the references as the minimum quality bar. You may improve: anatomical accuracy, rendering precision, background detail, lighting coherence, composition, typography accuracy, sharpness and storytelling clarity.

You may not: change the locked palette, linework, shading, character designs, panel conventions or lettering conventions; adopt a new fashionable style; or add new recurring visual devices. A proposed change to a LOCKED trait must be put to the operator as a recommendation, with reasons, and applied only if approved.

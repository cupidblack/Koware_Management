## 6. Owner decisions required before production activation

1. **Authority and effective versions:** Approve or reject master `CC-UPMP v0.09.01`, these candidate companion versions and CME compatibility; select effective date, revision, scope and rollback contact. A candidate is never automatically binding.
2. **Namespace/physical migration:** Either ratify the explicit physical-to-logical registry as permanent or authorize full reversible renaming with redirect/alias tests. Decide deployed `schema_version` separately.
3. **Calendar and historical archive:** Approve exact production timezone, chapter/phase start/end convention and partial/weekend/holiday exceptions, immutable ID and legacy `S00_T05`/`S00_CH05` relationship, archive root and Part compression rules.
4. **Eternal Dreamfall calendar:** Approve date-to-entry and day-to-passage allocation, religious-text sourcing and translation/quote rights, rather than assuming the 250-entry list implies 260 weeks of passages.
5. **People, canon, consent and mission:** Approve actually eligible canon, role chart, character/diary/likeness usage, Edubeam era and source boundaries; draft consent never means public consent.
6. **B/C security and storage:** Approve A/B physically separated destination/ACL, C answer vault, restricted export, transfer policy, retention/redaction and audit procedure; identify custodians.
7. **Editorial workflow:** Identify who can accept an A, approve continuity event changes, activate comic/media output, final titles, challenge reveals and release; acceptance and commit/release remain separate.
8. **QA and regression:** Execute controlled calendar, projection, migration and privacy tests with traceable fixtures plus actual authorized service trials. Static pass cannot be used as proof of live system readiness.

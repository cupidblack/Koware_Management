### 1.1 Mandatory entry and scope checks

Resolve the exact command, authorized actor, purpose, output audiences, target local civil date or authorized date-selection mechanism, effective production timezone, calendar and inclusive/exclusive chapter boundary convention, governing master/protocol/CME/ledger editions, latest accepted episode/story revision, access rights and permitted A/B destinations. ### 1.2 Stop/continue decisions

- `GO`: essential day/identity, authority, canon, accepted pre-state and output rights established for a *draft*, with no unresolved blocking dependency.
- `GO_WITH_OMISSIONS`: the authorized story remains safe and causally coherent after **documented nonessential** evidence is excluded. State what is omitted and which scene/claim it would otherwise affect.
- `BLOCKED`: definitive day identity, essential accepted mission/character causality, critical portrayal rights, mandatory evidence, secure A/B partition or the terminal challenge cannot be supported. Provide a **nonpublishable blocker report**, not an invented complete episode.
- `REVIEW_ONLY`: static document work is permitted even if operational real-time sources, private materials, user calendar or renderer are inaccessible. No story-mode run is inferred.

An owner's expressly limited **provisional private draft** may carry editorial uncertainties with explicit warning; it cannot legalize unlicensed copying, restricted-data disclosure or unsupported claims as fact. Failed live endpoints may be replaced only by genuinely retrieved alternatives or clearly dated user-supplied snapshots, recording original acquisition time, freshness and use limits. Never pretend a cached result is new telemetry.

Map `block_day` 0–4 to chronicle slots 1–5; 7–11 to slots 6–10; `episode_index=block+1`; `chapter_workday=block*10+chronicle_slot` (1–40).

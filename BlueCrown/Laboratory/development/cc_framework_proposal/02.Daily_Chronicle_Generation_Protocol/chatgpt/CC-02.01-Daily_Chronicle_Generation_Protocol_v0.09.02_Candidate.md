> **Operator directive.** This runbook operationalizes the governing master for one daily Chronicle. It does not amend, replace, summarize away or outrank CC-UPMP. Resolve the actual effective authority and commissioned mode first; retrieve only relevant evidence; establish the accepted continuity pre-state; run the six cumulative creative transformations using the eight-operation semantic merge loop; write one coherent story; prepare a physically separate internal handover; execute Q01–Q16; and report the real process, editorial, persistence, release and derivative states. Never invent missing source facts, approvals, IDs, events, storage operations or rendered assets.

## 1. Purpose, precedence and boundaries

### 1.1 Authority

Apply the precedence and truth rules in CC-UPMP §§2–5. In this runbook, the master governs franchise purpose, request routing, calendar, three clocks, source/rights boundaries, six transformations, Output A/B contracts, Q gates and release limitations. This protocol governs daily operator sequencing, evidence capture, stop/continue decisions and execution records. CC-05.01 governs semantic context assimilation. CC-02.02 governs logical continuity records and B6. CC-02.04 reports documentary and operational QA.

A newer candidate filename does not prove owner adoption. If the effective master differs from v0.09.01, use the actually authorized edition and record the discrepancy. If a companion conflicts with the master, follow the master within its authority and log the conflict. No document in this package authorizes publication, canon promotion, a physical schema migration, calendar mutation, repository write, answer-key creation or media production.

### 1.2 Entry condition

Run this protocol only for an explicit `DAILY_CHRONICLE` commission or a specifically authorized dry run. A request to review or refactor this protocol is `REVIEW_ONLY`, not a daily story commission. Route `DAILY_REVISION`, `FULL_DERIVATION_REVIEW`, `EPISODE_COMPILATION`, `COMIC_*`, `MEDIA_*`, `ARCHIVE_WRITE`, `CANON_PROMOTION`, `PUBLISH` and `REWARD_OPERATION` to their distinct master-defined modes.

A normal Chronicle is one authorized working day, ordinarily Monday–Friday. A weekend requires a separate `SPECIAL_WEEKEND` commission and never consumes a weekday slot by implication. Day #10 does not automatically compile or approve an episode. A daily run does not automatically generate comics, video, audio, a Service Card, an answer key or a public release.

### 1.3 Independent status axes

Record independently:

- Process: `REQUEST_CLASSIFIED`, `PREFLIGHT`, `GO`, `GO_WITH_OMISSIONS`, `BLOCKED`, `EVIDENCE_LOCKED_FOR_DRAFT`, `DRAFTED`, `AWAITING_EDITORIAL_REVIEW`.
- Editorial: `UNREVIEWED`, `CHANGES_REQUESTED`, `ACCEPTED`, `LOCKED`, `REJECTED`.
- Persistence: `UNSAVED`, `DRAFTED_NOT_COMMITTED`, `WRITE_AUTHORIZED`, `COMMIT_ATTEMPTED`, `COMMITTED_VERIFIED`, `WRITE_FAILED`, `CONFLICT_REQUIRES_RECONCILIATION`.
- Release: `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `RELEASE_ELIGIBLE`, `PUBLICATION_AUTHORIZED`, `PUBLISHED_VERIFIED`.
- Derivative: `NOT_COMMISSIONED`, `PLANNED`, `STORYBOARDED`, `PROMPTS_CREATED`, `ASSETS_RENDERED_VERIFIED`, `ASSEMBLED_VERIFIED`, `QA_PASSED`, `APPROVED`, `STALE_SOURCE`.

`NOT_RUN`, `NOT_CHECKED`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `NOT_APPLICABLE` are not passes. Do not collapse these axes into “complete”.

## 2. Inputs and required preflight decisions

Before research, create a compact run header containing:

1. Requested production date and timezone as stated by the user or authorized calendar.
2. Invocation time and timezone, separately from the story date.
3. Requested primary mode and intended audience.
4. Effective master edition and evidence of its authority; otherwise label it candidate-only.
5. Calendar authority, episode/day identity and legacy identifier status.
6. Previous accepted A revision and accepted continuity/event head, if available.
7. Character, mission, physical-location and knowledge states that are causally relevant.
8. Source/diary/likeness/quotation permissions and audience restrictions.
9. Requested deliverables and explicitly excluded side effects.
10. Missing inputs, material conflicts, decision owner and safe disposition.

Do not invent a missing date, timezone, episode number, chapter day, calendar, source permission, prior revision or accepted event head. If one essential fact is missing, ask one focused question when the user can supply it; otherwise apply `BLOCKED` or `GO_WITH_OMISSIONS` as defined by the master.

## 3. Phase 0 — classify, scope and authorize

1. Select exactly one primary mode.
2. Confirm the request is a daily Chronicle, not an episode outline, retrospective review or derivative commission.
3. Identify the requested story date, audience and delivery boundary.
4. Confirm whether the output is definitive, expressly provisional or blocked.
5. State that archive writes, calendar/task changes, publication, canon promotion, secure C creation and derivative rendering are not included unless separately commissioned and authorized.
6. Confirm no conflict with rights, privacy, law, provider rules or locked canon.

**Exit:** mode, date-resolution method, authority status, audience and output boundary are known. Otherwise stop and report the blocker.

## 4. Phase 1 — retrieve and freeze actual evidence

A URL, filename, search result, remembered excerpt or prior model assertion is not proof that the underlying source was read. Retrieve the actual relevant source and record, where available:

- canonical URL or record ID;
- repository branch/commit or document edition;
- source owner/custodian and authority class;
- actual retrieval timestamp with offset;
- source event date, publication date and retrieval date separately;
- source location and temporal scope;
- permission/license, audience and permitted use;
- relevant excerpt/claim and confidence;
- observed fields, missing fields, errors and fallback age;
- digest only if actually computed;
- narrative function and disposition.

Use evidence classes from CC-UPMP: `VERIFIED_REAL_WORLD_FACT`, `HISTORICAL_INTERPRETATION`, `CANON_ACCEPTED`, `FICTIONAL_DRAFT`, `SPECULATIVE_PROBE_SCENARIO`, `OPERATIONAL_PROPOSAL`, `UNVERIFIED`, `RESTRICTED` and `CONTRADICTED`.

### 4.1 Evidence domains

Evaluate the eight domains in the master’s source-acquisition contract:

1. Eternal Dreamfall Bible Study: actual reading-plan entry, effective date-to-week anchor, edition and permitted passage.
2. Eternal Dreamfall Chronicle: actual dated YouVersion verse or honestly attributed alternative provider, date and translation.
3. Refreshed Eternal Dreamfall: actual Buzzjuice Health & Safety course/module/lesson and applicable guidance.
4. Headlined Eternal Dreamfall: separately sourced historical, story-present and probe/future streams; event, publication and retrieval times kept distinct.
5. Classies Eternal Dreamfall: accepted earlier Chronicles, approved character/relationship continuity and consent-qualified diary use.
6. Classies Chronicles: verified Koware roles, projects and operational context transformed into fiction without claiming fictional work happened in reality.
7. Approved canon and mission records: character-specific era limits, accepted locations, Edubeam capabilities, mission roster, knowledge and obligations.
8. Authorized schedules/tasks/other context: only actually connected or supplied records within permitted access scope.

Research can be retained in B2 without being forced into the public story. A context belongs in the story only if it materially changes a choice, stake, obstacle, consequence, atmosphere, interpretation, timing, imagery or resolution.

### 4.2 Source fallback

Distinguish `NOT_FETCHED`, `FETCHED_UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFIED_AUTHORIZED`, `ACCESS_RESTRICTED`, `SOURCE_DOWN`, `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `CONFLICTING` and `NOT_APPLICABLE`. A fallback must identify its real provider, date, translation/edition and limitations. A cached endpoint response is an as-of snapshot, not a new live reading. A future Probe timestamp is not future journalism or verified future weather.

## 5. Phase 2 — resolve calendar, clocks and identities

Apply CC-UPMP §§7–10 and the actual approved calendar. Verify the target weekday, phase membership, inclusive/exclusive phase boundary, complete episode fit, episode slot #1–#10 and chapter-wide role-check day #1–#40. A role-check day is not the date of the month or episode slot.

Do not derive the calendar from a Probe endpoint. Do not assign an ordinary weekday number to a weekend, gap, unapproved partial block or ambiguous phase. Preserve existing canonical and legacy IDs; do not infer a new archive path or equate `T05` with `CH05`.

Reconcile the three clocks independently:

- **P — production/present:** target story date/time and authorized production timezone.
- **H — historical/reference:** actual historical endpoint value and the distinct historical reference location.
- **F — field/probe:** actual captured Probe destination, phase and mission state.

For every featured or deployed character, record accepted physical location, local time, travel/transport state, knowledge and evidence source. Distinguish HQ/control, lab, Enka’s physical present, Enka’s historical reference and each deployed participant. A dream, simulation, memory, remote message and physical transfer are different modalities. Never depict teleportation, reunion or return without an accepted causal event.

**Exit:** calendar identity and every story-critical clock/location are supported or explicitly qualified. A material contradiction blocks the affected definitive scene.

## 6. Phase 3 — accepted continuity and story design

Load the latest actually accepted parent A, accepted event head, effective character/world canon, mission state and open storylines. Record the source revision and base revision. Read accepted events in authorized revision/sequence order, not merely fictional timestamp order. Exclude proposed, rejected, revoked or unapproved events from accepted state.

Create a scene-purpose plan with:

- central dramatic question or desire;
- protagonist/spotlight perspective and reason for selection;
- opposition, risk or dilemma;
- relevant source contexts and intended transformation;
- accepted facts the story must preserve;
- choices and consequences;
- character knowledge boundaries;
- mission/location/technology constraints;
- open and closing story hooks;
- terminal public Challenge A design.

Choose role spotlights using the current authorized organization chart and the chapter-wide day 1–40 check. Normally one or two spotlight roles; expand only when the master and evidence justify it. Preserve distinction among `ACTUAL_VERIFIED`, `FICTIONAL_STORY` and `PROPOSAL_ONLY`.

## 7. Phase 4 — run the six cumulative creative transformations

The master’s six V1–V6 transformations are cumulative. The eight-operation semantic merge loop in CC-UPMP §19 and CC-05.01 applies at each version boundary. Never create six disconnected stories or append source summaries as detached paragraphs.

| Version | Exact name | New context duty | Completion test |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | Eternal Dreamfall Bible Study | Study the actual allocated reading and create the first story seed | Source literary anchors remain recognizable; study affects stakes, choice or interpretation |
| V2 | Eternal Dreamfall Chronicle | Assimilate the genuine dated verse as moral/emotional lens | A consequential choice or interpretation changes without erasing V1 |
| V3 | Refreshed Eternal Dreamfall | Introduce an actual practical Health & Safety issue | Hazard recognition/intervention changes risk or consequence |
| V4 | Headlined Eternal Dreamfall | Integrate selected verified historical/current evidence and clearly labeled speculative Probe material | Research affects pressure, context or decision without false factual claims |
| V5 | Classies Eternal Dreamfall | Assimilate accepted Classies continuity and permission-qualified personal/diary context | Character motivation and serial continuity deepen without unauthorized disclosure |
| V6 | Classies Chronicles | Integrate Koware role/project life, Edubeam and the other retained contexts into one original story | One coherent, consequential, entertaining Chronicle with earned outcome and open continuity |

At each boundary, execute: **READ** actual source and scope; **DECOMPOSE** claims, chronology, actions, motives and risks; **LOCK** hard/soft/optional story anchors; **CONNECT** the strongest legitimate bridge; **TRANSFORM** affected scenes; **PRESERVE** indispensable source anchors and chronology; **VALIDATE** facts, canon, rights, clocks and coherence; **REGISTER** source, changed scene, retained anchors, consequence, exclusions and status.

The ordinary daily output is V6 only. Full V1–V6 prose is reserved for a separate `FULL_DERIVATION_REVIEW` commission. Keep internal derivation notes in B2/B5, not as six public installments.

## 8. Phase 5 — draft Output A

Output A is the standalone public-eligible story draft. It must:

- be one original, coherent Chronicle with a beginning, escalation, meaningful choice, consequence and earned ending;
- preserve accepted continuity and character knowledge;
- make researched contexts causally or thematically active rather than mechanically inserting them;
- distinguish real evidence from fiction through internal provenance, not clumsy exposition in story prose;
- avoid unsupported claims about actual Koware work, real people, private diaries, science or future events;
- honor character-specific canon, mission position, era technology and physical location;
- end with exactly one eligible public Challenge A;
- contain no answer, correct option, solution rationale, revealing near-hint or answer-bearing metadata;
- contain no footer, bibliography, timestamp, internal note, signature, B heading, credit or explanatory material after the terminal challenge unless the master’s applicable public contract expressly permits it before the challenge.

The challenge must be fair and solvable from permitted audience material. It must not require unauthorized access to private records or compel disclosure of personal data, unsafe activity or unconsented filming.

If the story is safe as a draft but competitive release depends on unavailable secure answer custody, the story may remain `DRAFTED` while competitive release remains blocked. Do not create restricted C in an ordinary daily run.

## 9. Phase 6 — prepare physically separate Output B1–B8

Output B is internal-only. It is not appended to the public story and must be stored/transferred separately with audience and sensitivity controls. Use the exact master ordering:

| **B2 — Research/source register.** | Eight evidence domains, V1–V6 provenance, claims, citations, rights, fallback, omissions and conflicts | `research_register`, `source_snapshot_register`, `source_permission_register` |
| **B3 — Character, Koware role and project register.** | Character canon/rights, chart day 1–40, alias/role evidence, diary status and actual-vs-fictional-vs-proposal work | `character_state_record`, `role_project_log`, `source_permission_register`, `editorial_decision_log` |
| **B4 — Mission and technology state.** | Roster, physical/temporal coordinates, accepted Edubeam capabilities, power, communications, equipment, risks and retrieval obligations | `mission_state_record`, `continuity_event_log` |
| **B5 — Append-only continuity and story ledger.** | Accepted pre-state, scene-bound proposed deltas, hypothetical post-state, storylines, knowledge and pending canon proposals | `continuity_event_log`, `storyline_register`, `character_state_record` |
| **B6 — Portable production handover.** | Complete fifteen-part self-contained safe handover | `handover_record` and permission-filtered source/event refs |
| **B7 — Derivative/media and challenge status.** | Public challenge reference and release obligation; opaque key custody status; actual commissioned assets, dependencies, rights and staleness | `challenge_public_register`, `production_manifest`, `service_card_register`, `source_permission_register` |
| **B8 — Validation, exceptions and approval register.** | Q01–Q16, evidence, results, blockers, omissions, decision owners, approvals, write/readback and release truth | `validation_report`, `exception_conflict_register`, `editorial_decision_log`, `handover_record` |

Use `NO_CHANGE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `NOT_RUN`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `UNKNOWN` precisely, with reasons. Do not invent inactive records to fill sections. Preserve historical B3 role/project and B7 permissions/editorial evidence through the mapping above. Do not add a ninth B section.

If secure answer custody is absent, record exactly: `RESTRICTED OUTPUT NOT CREATED; HUMAN SECURE CUSTODY REQUIRED`. No solution, answer letter or revealing rationale appears in A or B.

## 10. Phase 7 — exact daily quality gates Q01–Q16

Every gate receives `PASS`, `FAIL`, `NOT_RUN` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, evidence reference, reviewer/actor, timestamp and remediation. `NOT_RUN` is not a pass. `BLOCKED` is recorded as an unresolved blocking issue; `NOT_CHECKED` maps to `NOT_RUN`.

| Gate | Test |
|---|---|
| `Q01_SCOPE` | One requested mode/date, authorized output count and bounded actions |
| `Q02_CALENDAR` | Approved calendar/timezone, eligible weekday, phase fit, episode #1–#10 and chapter day #1–#40 |
| `Q03_THREE_CLOCKS` | Independent P/H/F evidence, timestamps, locations and modality |
| `Q04_IDENTIFIERS` | Existing canonical/legacy IDs and archive paths preserved |
| `Q05_CANON` | Specific character/era/Edubeam limits, relationships, roster and accepted past events |
| `Q06_ROLE_PROJECT` | Correct chapter-day chart, sourced alias/current work, actual/fiction/proposal separation |
| `Q07_STUDY` | Genuine reading plan, approved effective anchor/selection or truthful unresolved status |
| `Q08_VERSE_HS` | Real verse provider/date/translation and actual H&S source/lesson or disclosed omission |
| `Q09_RESEARCH` | Event/publication/retrieval chronology; verified history/present facts; future labeled fictional |
| `Q10_RIGHTS` | Specific-use permission, consent/revocation, private material excluded and export eligibility |
| `Q11_STORY` | One cohesive original story with consequential choices and cumulative contexts |
| `Q12_CHALLENGE` | Fair terminal question; no answer or near-answer leakage |
| `Q13_RECORDS` | Separate B1–B8, append-only event history, portable B6 and truthful statuses |
| `Q14_DERIVATIVES` | No uncommissioned assets; correct source revisions and stale flags |
| `Q15_INTEGRITY` | Every read, approval, write, render and publish claim supported by real evidence |
| `Q16_BOUNDARIES` | Public/internal/restricted separation; no answer key in ordinary output |

A failed mandatory gate cannot be offset by a high average. Repair at the smallest responsible layer. A fatal rights/privacy/safety issue blocks the affected depiction or release, not necessarily every safe portion of the draft.

## 11. Phase 8 — editorial, persistence and release disposition

Without separate authorization, finish at `DRAFTED_NOT_COMMITTED` and `AWAITING_EDITORIAL_REVIEW`; release is `NOT_ELIGIBLE` or `RELEASE_ELIGIBLE` according to evidence, not presumed publication authority. If a separately authorized write is requested, compare the actual base revision, preserve concurrency, execute the authorized operation, obtain commit/receipt and read back the resulting artifact. If no write tool/capability exists, say so.

`ACCEPTED`, `LOCKED`, `COMMITTED_VERIFIED`, `PUBLICATION_AUTHORIZED` and `PUBLISHED_VERIFIED` each require their own real evidence. Accepting prose does not accept continuity deltas, promote canon, grant rights or authorize publication. A derivative is not commissioned by a daily Chronicle request.

## 12. Phase 9 — handover and next-day readiness

B6 must allow a fresh provider to reconstruct the accepted state without relying on prior conversation memory. Distinguish accepted recap from today's draft, preserve proposed changes as proposals, list open decisions and state the next eligible working day only if the approved calendar proves it. Do not assume a daily draft has been accepted.

At Chronicle #10, report ten individual day slots and their real revision/acceptance states. This is compilation readiness only. It does not automatically create the episode compilation, approve its title, retrieve a mission team or release a challenge answer.

## 13. Required B6 portable handover — fifteen elements

1. Identity and revisions: season/chapter/episode/day IDs, legacy crosswalk, A revision, editorial state, owner reference and effective master/protocol/ledger editions.
2. Calendar: requested local production date, approved timezone, weekday, episode slot #1–#10, chapter day #1–#40 where verified, calendar authority and fit/exception.
3. Temporal/spatial snapshot: P/H/F values, evidence ages, HQ, Enka-present, Enka-historical, lab, Probe destination and each deployed person's position.
4. What happened: last accepted story recap separate from today's draft, causal consequences and story revision references.
5. Character projection: location/time, knowledge, relationship, obligations, permitted injury data, era capability, rights and accepted/proposed distinction.
6. Mission projection: roster, temporal coordinates, device charge/comms, last accepted contact, equipment/risk and outstanding retrieval.
7. Organizational chart/project: chart revision, chapter day, featured roles and actual/fictional/proposal classification.
8. Study and practical learning: reading anchor, selected work, verse provider/date/translation and actual H&S course/lesson.
9. Research provenance: verified history/current reporting versus `FICTION_PROBE_SCENARIO`, source dates, geography/weather and omissions.
10. Storyline/event changes: open/advanced/resolved/deferred threads, accepted event refs, proposed changes and hypothetical post-state.
11. Challenge: nonrevealing public question reference, A revision, prior release obligation and opaque restricted-custody status only.
12. Derivatives/cards: actual commissioned assets, source bindings, rights, `CURRENT`/`STALE_SOURCE`/`NOT_COMMISSIONED`.
13. Open exceptions/decisions: missing evidence, conflicts, severity, decision owner and resolution requirement.
14. Next eligible working day: accepted opening position, plausible opportunities, knowledge/mission constraints and refresh tasks.
15. Operational truth: actual A/B paths, write/readback/commit evidence, editorial/lock/release state and recipient permissions.

## 14. Stop conditions and safe partial progress

Stop or block the affected scope when essential calendar identity, causal pre-state, rights, safety, character canon or audience separation cannot be established. Continue with omissions only when missing material is genuinely noncentral and the remaining story is still truthful and coherent. Preserve safe research and a nonpublishable draft where allowed. Never turn a provisional workaround into a definitive factual claim.

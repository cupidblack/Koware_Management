## 3. Master v0.09.01 compatibility requirements

The updated companions must preserve these master-level invariants:

1. The master is governing; a higher version number or repository presence is not adoption.
2. One daily commission produces one Output A and separate internal Output B1–B8.
3. V1–V6 are six cumulative transformations; ordinary output is the final coherent V6, not six detached stories.
4. The semantic merge uses the master’s eight-operation loop: read, decompose, lock, connect, transform, preserve, validate, register.
5. P/H/F clocks, each character's physical location, accepted mission state and character knowledge are independent facts.
6. Calendar authority, episode slot #1–#10 and chapter role day #1–#40 are separate.
7. Exactly Q01–Q16 are the daily gate identifiers.
8. Exactly B1–B8 are the internal handover sections; B6 contains fifteen mandatory elements.
9. The nineteen ECLPM family names remain stable; `calendar_probe_record` remains a compatibility alias, not a twentieth family.
10. Accepted continuity is built from accepted events only. Draft events remain proposed.
11. Editorial acceptance, canon promotion, persistence, publication and derivative approval are independent.
12. No answer key, secure C, uncommissioned comic/video, repository write or public release is implied.

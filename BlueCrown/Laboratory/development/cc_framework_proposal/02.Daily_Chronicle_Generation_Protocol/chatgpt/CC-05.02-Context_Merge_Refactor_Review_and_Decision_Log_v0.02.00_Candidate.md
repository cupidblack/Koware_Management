## 2. Findings from the previous specialist review

The following decisions remain sound and are retained:

- Semantic assimilation, not concatenation, is the core objective.
- The original story's hard anchors must survive while its significance may change.
- A source must materially affect stakes, choice, obstacle, agency, consequence, atmosphere, interpretation or closure.
- A source can be valid research but rejected for the public story.
- Future Probe material is fictional scenario material, not verified news.
- Every source has date, rights, confidence and audience boundaries.
- Diary access does not establish public permission.
- Fictional Koware action is not proof of real company work.
- A curriculum outline is not evidence that a specific lesson was completed.
- A full V1–V6 derivation is a separate mode; ordinary daily output is one final V6 story.

## 3. Important factual and operational corrections

### 3.1 Reading-plan content must be checked literally

A correct week calculation is not enough. The actual assigned line in the current reading-plan file must be read and attributed accurately. The specialist must not infer book groupings from a previous model answer or a week number alone. The review that identified the Week 98 mismatch remains a useful regression fixture, but the current effective date anchor and plan version must be revalidated at run time.

### 3.2 Context source classes are not interchangeable

A historical record, current report, future Probe timestamp, speculative scenario, diary entry, organizational proposal and accepted canon event are different kinds of evidence. Their timestamps, authority and permissions cannot be normalized into a single generic “context” field.

### 3.3 Preserve source identity and story identity separately

The source may inspire a scene without being literally reproduced in the story world. The fox/dog teaching example is a merge-quality fixture: the original action remains recognizable while the new moral/emotional lens changes its significance. It is not permission to anthropomorphize real animals as factual claim or to copy protected expression.

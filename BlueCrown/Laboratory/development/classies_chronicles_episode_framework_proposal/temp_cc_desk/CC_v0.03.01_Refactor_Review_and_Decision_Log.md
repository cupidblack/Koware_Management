## 1. Review conclusion

The original material combines a distinctive worldbuilding premise with unusually detailed scheduling, corporate operations, biblical research, temporal-probe reporting and production controls. Its main weakness is not missing creativity but **mixed layers of authority**: some lines specify story canon, others are illustrative daily routines, news-research quotas, archival examples, real-world requests and ideas for future integrations. Across the platform responses, similar instructions recur with contradictory details. A literal concatenation would multiply duplication and make AI execution inconsistent.

The v0.03.01 rewrite therefore creates **one stand-alone executable master prompt** while preserving the named responsibilities of the existing framework, daily protocol and ledger specification. It separates narrative from source research, live temporal telemetry, restricted challenge keys, continuity records and post-chronicle media. The result is a new **proposal**, not an assertion that governance changes have already been approved.

**Episode Continuity Ledger v0.02.00:** Preserved episode manifest, research register, permissions, mission and event log, challenge/answer separation, production manifest, daily handover, publication states, optional Service Cards, portable cross-provider context and human-controlled compilation.

**Platform response compilation 1:** Considered AI Desk/Studio architectural separation, additional storyline/three-layer design, Edubeam law proposals, first-class challenge behavior, production and diagram/shot concepts, release and calendar reconciliation, staged integrations and human approval gates. Useful recommendations are implemented without adopting speculative figures as fact.

**Platform response compilation 2:** Considered the three-time-axis architecture, deterministic calendar arithmetic, six-stage research pipeline, evolving episode day rhythm, 3-global/5-local news curation, multimedia timing, comic-facing adaptation rules, provisional v0.03.00 master-prompt treatments, privacy/canon warnings and previous platform limitations. The organizational chart itself is not proof of consent to depict named persons, nor is a list of weeks proof of a specific calendar anchor.

## 4. Recommended controlled adoption

1. **Editorial review:** Evaluate the proposed v0.03.01 prompt and its decision register; record the approved baseline, exceptions and person/role authorized to sign off.
2. **Canonical contracts:** Ratify phase-date calculation, season/chapter/episode naming, Bible plan mapping, Studio/AI Desk versions, primary character/Edubeam scope and challenge-answer storage.
3. **One-episode pilot:** Generate ten weekdays separately, maintain a continuously updated ledger and source/provenance record, and test a cross-provider handover with no hidden conversation history.
4. **Publication pilot:** Compile manually, verify written/storyboard/media consistency and that the final public beat is the challenge while its key remains restricted.
5. **Technical staging:** Only after a successful pilot, consider archive indexing, calendar/diary reads, controlled reminder writes, Service Card/43 Clues/rewards integrations and automated validation.

## 5. Residual limitations

The core listed documents and long platform compilations were available for review, but not every underlying supplementary repository page or named Studio Blueprint/AI Desk version was independently resolved in this pass. Their precise approval state, archived filenames, permissions and detailed schemas therefore remain subject to live project authority. This review does **not** claim to have performed a publication, repository migration, calendar integration, consent audit, live probe run or production of an actual daily chronicle. The master prompt is nevertheless a complete, actionable proposed procedure: it requires real retrieved values at execution time rather than embedding false example data.

Act as the Classies Chronicles audiovisual showrunner, narrative adaptation screenwriter, episode/shot planner, visual continuity supervisor, motion director, AI video prompt engineer, sound designer, narration editor, accessibility coordinator, post-production editor, rights checker, quality assurance reviewer and handover author.

The final media deliverable must stand alone as a complete narrative unit, integrate seamlessly as an insert segment within the Classies Chronicles Studio podcast (`Studio_Structure_Skeletal_Blueprint_v22.2` §29), or be adapted to authorized vertical social cuts.

The production goal is a rich cinematic experience rather than an automated slideshow of static illustrations. Achieve dynamic storytelling through motivated camera movement, purposeful lens selection, narrated causality, expressive character action, immersive soundscapes, disciplined scene transitions, and selective motion generation. Strictly preserve the distinction between verified real-world reporting and speculative future-world science fiction. Never invent unapproved EduBeam mechanics, corporate actions, character relationships or scientific breakthroughs merely to fill running time. Where visual drama requires additional duration, deepen observation, atmosphere and character reactions already implicit in the authorized source.

# PART II — DRAMATURGY, VISUAL CONTINUITY AND ACT MOTION TAXONOMY

## 7. Terminology Hierarchy: Story, Page, Scene, Act, Beat, Shot and Clip

To ensure precision across production disciplines, enforce these non-interchangeable definitions:

- **Source Story Unit:** A discrete narrative paragraph or causal event in the authorized Public Chronicle (`Output A`).
- **Comic Page:** A sequential-art composition (default 9:16 portrait in `CC-03.01 v0.08.00`, containing 1 to 4 panels).
- **Video Scene:** A unified dramatic unit of narrative action occurring in a single physical location and time period. One comic page may provide elements for a scene, but a scene is not structurally restricted to one page.
- **Act:** A modular, time-budgeted audiovisual segment serving an explicit dramatic function (e.g., 10 seconds in a 16-act plan).
- **Beat:** An atomic dramatic change within an act (a discovery, realization, choice, physical action, or consequence).
- **Shot:** An unbroken visual composition and camera capture interval. An act may consist of one primary shot or two coordinated shorter shots.
- **Clip:** An actual rendered or recorded media file (video or audio) on disk.
- **Sequence:** A series of assembled scenes forming a complete story arc.
- **Episode / Podcast Segment:** The broader container program in which the chronicle sequence is embedded.

## 8. Comic-to-Video Cropping and Framing Protocol (9:16 to 16:9)

Classies Chronicles comics are created as **9:16 vertical pages** for mobile readability (`CC-03.01 v0.08.00`), while the master video derivative defaults to **16:9 widescreen** (`1920 × 1080 px`). When adapting vertical comic panels into widescreen video frames, apply one of four standardized framing modes based on narrative context:

```text
[ 9:16 Vertical Comic Panel ] 
         │
         ├─── Mode 1: Focused Cinematic Horizontal Crop (Pan & Scan Focal Extraction)
         ├─── Mode 2: AI Background Outpainting / Horizontal Extension (1920x1080)
         ├─── Mode 3: Dynamic Multi-Panel / Split-Screen Widescreen Composite
         └─── Mode 4: Centered Pillarbox with Ambient Blurred Wings
```

### Mode 1: Focused Cinematic Horizontal Crop (Pan & Scan Focal Extraction)
- **Application:** Close-up character acting, emotional beats, detail inserts, and intimate dialogue.
- **Execution:** Extract a native 16:9 rectangular window ($1920 \times 1080$ aspect ratio) centered on the primary dramatic subject (e.g., character's eyes, facial expression, handheld prop). Ensure the vertical crop does not truncate chins, foreheads, or vital action cues.
- **Result:** Pure cinematic widescreen framing with maximum character intimacy.

### Mode 2: AI Background Outpainting / Horizontal Canvas Extension
- **Application:** Wide establishing shots, laboratory environments, Accra streetscapes, and environmental action.
- **Execution:** Place the 9:16 comic panel or character subject in the center or along the rule-of-thirds of a $1920 \times 1080$ canvas. Use image outpainting (generative fill via Krea, Photoshop, Leonardo, or ComfyUI) to extend the physical environment horizontally to the left and right borders, maintaining consistent architectural style, lighting, and palette.
- **Result:** Seamless full-width 16:9 scene preserving complete character anatomy.

### Mode 3: Dynamic Split-Screen / Multi-Panel Comic Composite
- **Application:** Cross-cutting action, telephone/radio conversations, simultaneous reactions, or cause-and-effect beats.
- **Execution:** Arrange two or three complementary 9:16 panel crops side-by-side on the 16:9 canvas separated by stylized comic gutters (e.g., Left: Enka examining a flickering monitor; Right: Wide view of the Blue Crown server bank).
- **Result:** Graphic novel aesthetic perfectly suited for high-density storytelling.

### Mode 4: Centered Pillarbox with Ambient Blurred Wings
- **Application:** Static holds, historical data screens, full-page sequential art contemplation, or low-cost fallback.
- **Execution:** Place the complete, uncropped 9:16 comic panel in the center of the 16:9 canvas. Duplicate the panel, scale it to fill the entire $1920 \times 1080$ background, apply a heavy Gaussian blur ($40\text{–}60\text{px}$) and a 30% darkness vignette.
- **Result:** Professional broadcast presentation without black bars or distorted aspect ratios.

## 10. Studio Blueprint Act Motion Taxonomy

As advised in `Studio_Structure_Skeletal_Blueprint_v22.2` §27 and `v19.0` §49–50, do not attempt to generate a continuous 160-second or 200-second single AI video. Deconstruct the chronicle into modular, time-budgeted acts governed by five explicit architectural attributes:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        ACT ATTRIBUTES TAXONOMY                         │
├───────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ MOTION_CLASS      │ STATIONARY │ ANIMATED │ FULL_MOTION                │
│ PACING_TEMPO      │ CONTEMPLATIVE │ INVESTIGATIVE │ TENSE │ KINETIC    │
│ CAMERA_GRAMMAR    │ LOCKED │ PUSH_IN │ PULL_OUT │ TRACKING │ ORBIT     │
│ VFX_LEVEL         │ CLEAN │ ATMOSPHERIC │ EDUBEAM_SURGE │ GLITCH       │
│ AUDIO_PROFILE     │ NARRATOR │ DIALOGUE │ AMBIENCE │ SCORE_SWELL       │
└───────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. `MOTION_CLASS: STATIONARY` (Static Hold & High-Detail Focus)
- **Cinematic Treatment:** Static, locked camera hold on a high-resolution cropped comic graphic or detailed data frame. Zero subject or background warping. Subtle lighting pulsation, ambient monitor flicker, or slow atmospheric dust motes.
- **Production Role:** Reserved for deep analytical reflections, complex technical/educational displays, and the terminal audience challenge. Completely eliminates generative AI artifacts for critical informational moments.

### 2. `MOTION_CLASS: ANIMATED` (2.5D Parallax & NLE Keyframe Motion)
- **Cinematic Treatment:** Multi-plane parallax animation executed in CapCut Desktop or lightweight motion tools (Leonardo Motion, Haiper, Pika). Foreground characters separated from backgrounds; subtle Ken Burns zoom (1.0 to 1.08 scale), lateral camera drift, natural eye blink, breathing cycles, or atmospheric smoke drift.
- **Production Role:** Standard narrative continuity, character reaction shots, and environmental scene transitions. High visual elegance with zero risk of anatomical morphing.

### 3. `MOTION_CLASS: FULL_MOTION` (AI Generative Video)
- **Cinematic Treatment:** Complex generative video clips produced via Tier 1/2 engines (Kling AI, Luma Dream Machine, MiniMax Hailuo, Runway Gen-3). Fluid character locomotion, physical interaction with objects, volumetric lighting shifts, vehicle movement through traffic, or EduBeam plasma discharge.
- **Production Role:** High-impact dramatic climaxes, physical confrontations, and perceptual anomalies. Used selectively to maximize impact while conserving platform credit quotas.

# PART III — TIMING ARITHMETIC, BUDGETS AND PLATFORM ROUTING

## 11. Duration Arithmetic and Timecode Integrity

Duration is an exact mathematical contract. Differentiate:
1. **Raw Generation Duration:** Duration of individual generated clips (typically 5s or 10s).
2. **Assembled Timeline Duration:** Exact edit length of the scene/act after trimming and transition overlaps.
3. **Container Program Duration:** The broader podcast or broadcast running time.

The timeline duration formula is:

$$\text{FINAL\_DURATION} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} d_i + \sum b + t + c + r - \text{approved overlaps}$$

Where $d_i$ are act durations, $b$ are audio/visual scene bridges, $t$ is title card, $c$ is terminal challenge, and $r$ are pre-challenge credits.

### The Two Approved Timing Models:
- **Model A — Daily 160-Second Master (4-Scene / 16-Act Standard):**
  - 4 Narrative Scenes $\times$ 4 Acts per scene = **16 Acts**.
  - Duration per Act = **10.0 seconds** (or two coordinated 5.0s clips).
  - Total Duration = $16 \times 10.0\text{s} = \mathbf{160.0\text{ seconds (02:40.000)}}$.
- **Model B — Studio Podcast Segment Master (4-Scene / 20-Act Pilot Blueprint):**
  - 4 Narrative Scenes $\times$ 5 Acts per scene = **20 Acts**.
  - Duration per Act = **10.0 seconds**.
  - Total Duration = $20 \times 10.0\text{s} = \mathbf{200.0\text{ seconds (03:20.000)}}$.

### Phase 0: Preflight, Source Lock & Quota Verification

- Generate video clips act-by-act using the Dual-Reference Prompt Architecture.

### Phase 6: Voice Recording, Dialogue Lip-Sync & Audio Mix
- Record narrator voiceover in REAPER or generate voice tracks using approved TTS voices.

- Place video clips and stills on the main video track matching the exact 10.0s timecode intervals.
- Apply subtle keyframed zooms, smooth cross-dissolves (0.5s–1.0s), and unified color grading.
- Insert pre-challenge production credits in Act 15.

### Phase 8: Studio Podcast / OBS Playback Integration
- For standalone daily releases, export the master MP4 file.
- For Studio podcast integration, export an uncompressed master, load it into OBS Studio as a dedicated Media Source, and rehearse audio routing through the Studio communications bus (`Blueprint v22.2` §28).

### Phase 9: Subtitling, Accessibility & Title-Safe Verification
- Generate closed captions from the final recorded audio track; verify spelling of proper nouns and names.

- Verify that subtitles do not obscure the terminal challenge text in Act 16.

### Phase 10: Technical Export, M01–M30 Audit & Production Handover
- Export master file: MP4 container, H.264 video codec, AAC stereo audio, 1920×1080, 24fps, target bitrate 15–20 Mbps.
- Execute full audit against gates `M01–M30`; verify file playback from first frame to last frame.
- Compile the 15-Fact Handover Package and commit metadata to `production_manifest`.
- Submit deliverable to the Human Editor for final broadcast authorization.

# 1. EXECUTIVE DETERMINATION AND MANDATE

**Retain, elevate, and comprehensively refactor the specialized Classies Chronicles Master Media Generation Prompt to candidate version v0.08.00.**

The previous iteration (`CC-04.01 v0.04.00`) established essential architectural foundations: distinguishing media adaptation from story authoring, separating planning from execution, establishing duration arithmetic, and defining initial quality assurance gates. However, the subsequent evolution of the entire Classies Chronicles technical production set to version `v0.08.00` (`CC-01.01` through `CC-03.03`), combined with specific production directives from the user and multi-platform engineering reviews, necessitated a comprehensive refactor.

### Key Drivers for the v0.08.00 Refactor:
1. **Universal Suite Synchronization:** Aligning CC-04 directly with the ratified `v0.08.00` master baseline (`CC-UPMP v0.08.00`), daily protocol (`CC-DCGP v0.08.00`), episode continuity ledger (`CC-ECLPM v0.08.00`), and mobile-first master comic specification (`CC-MCGP v0.08.00`).
2. **Top 20 Free Refreshing Credit Platforms (Note 2):** Providing an exhaustive, operationally verified matrix of the top 20 AI video generation platforms offering recurring free credits (daily login check-ins, recurring fast-token pools, and monthly quotas), complete with clip durations, I2V controls, aspect ratios, licensing tiers, and failover routing.
3. **Copy-Paste Prompt Engineering Protocol (Note 3):** Establishing a standardized, fully populated natural-language syntax for both Image-to-Video (I2V) and Text-to-Video (T2V) that embeds verified narrative beats, character canon visual anchors, and camera cinematography while eliminating empty placeholders.
4. **Comic-to-Video Cropping Pipeline (Note 4):** Resolving the geometric tension between the 9:16 vertical comic standard (`CC-03.01 v0.08.00`) and the 16:9 widescreen video master through four explicit framing modes: Mode 1 (Pan & Scan Horizontal Crop), Mode 2 (Outpainted Canvas Extension), Mode 3 (Dynamic Multi-Panel Composite), and Mode 4 (Pillarbox with Ambient Blurred Wings).
5. **Character Canon Visual Continuity (Note 5):** Enforcing mandatory preflight identification and injection of official character media from `CC_AI_DESK/04_CANON/CHARACTERS/<CHARACTER_ID>/` (specifically `Enka_Character_Reference_Collage.jpg` and `CUPID_BLACK_CHARACTER_CANON.md`), locking wardrobe, grooming, and era-gated ability boundaries.
6. **Studio Blueprint Act Motion Taxonomy (Note 6):** Formalizing the motion classifications (`STATIONARY`, `ANIMATED` / 2.5D Parallax, and `FULL_MOTION` / AI Generative Video) and act attributes (`PACING_TEMPO`, `CAMERA_GRAMMAR`, `VFX_LEVEL`, `AUDIO_PROFILE`, `INTEGRATION_SLOT`) advised in `Studio_Structure_Skeletal_Blueprint_v22.2` §27 and `v19.0` §49–50.
7. **Default 16:9 Widescreen Standard (Note 7):** Standardizing the master video derivative on **16:9 widescreen ($1920 \times 1080\text{ px}$)** for broadcast, podcast, and web playback, while preserving 9:16 vertical as an explicit secondary derivative (`DERIVATIVE_SHORTS` mode).

# 3. ARCHITECTURAL BOUNDARIES AND RESPONSIBILITY MODEL

To prevent scope creep and operational collisions, enforce strict jurisdictional boundaries across the system:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      SYSTEM RESPONSIBILITY MATRIX                      │
├─────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ COMPONENT           │ CORE RESPONSIBILITY & EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY        │
├─────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CC-UPMP (CC-01.01)  │ Story authority, canon boundaries, 3 clocks      │
│ CC-DCGP (CC-02.01)  │ Daily prose execution, source provenance         │
│ CC-ECLPM (CC-02.03) │ 19 canonical logical families, durable handover  │
│ CC-MCGP (CC-03.01)  │ Sequential art, mobile 9:16 layout, panel art    │
│ CC-MMGP (CC-04.01)  │ Audiovisual adaptation, 16:9 master, prompts, QA │
│ Studio Blueprint    │ Physical workstations, OBS/REAPER/CapCut routing │
│ Human Authority     │ Rights approval, budget, canon promotion, publish│
└─────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **CC-UPMP:** Dictates narrative causality, character permissions, calendar dates, and ethical boundaries. It does not generate video or manage NLE timelines.
2. **CC-DCGP:** Governs the generation of daily prose (`Output A`) and internal continuity packets (`Output B1–B8`). It does not trigger video rendering as a side effect.
3. **CC-ECLPM:** Governs durable episode-owned data across 19 canonical logical families. Media production writes its metadata strictly to `production_manifest` and reads safe references; it never creates a 20th record family.
4. **CC-MCGP:** Governs sequential art adaptation and outputs 9:16 comic pages. It does not dictate video scene counts or enforce video playback durations.
5. **CC-MMGP (This System):** Governs the transformation of authorized text and comic panels into time-budgeted audiovisual acts, video prompt packs, audio mixes, CapCut timelines, and broadcast exports. It never alters underlying story events or publishes media without human approval.
6. **Studio Blueprint v22.2:** Governs the studio recording environment, OBS scene switching, REAPER audio routing, and CapCut desktop responsibilities. It does not author character dialogue or invent narrative beats.
7. **Authorized Human Authority:** Owns budget approval, commercial rights releases, challenge secret custody, canon promotions, and final publication.

# 5. STUDIO BLUEPRINT v22.2 AND v19.0 COMPATIBILITY AUDIT

A rigorous comparative analysis was performed against `Studio_Structure_Skeletal_Blueprint_v22.2.txt` (the canonical system-level blueprint) and `Studio_Structure_Skeletal_Blueprint_v19.0.txt` (containing specific Cupid Black production pipelines):

### 5.1 The Cupid Black Production Model (Blueprint v19.0 §49–50)
Blueprint v19.0 explicitly establishes:
> *"Do not attempt to generate a continuous 200-second AI video. Use: 200 SECOND STORY $\to$ STORY BEATS $\to$ COMIC PANELS $\to$ CAMERA MOTION $\to$ SHORT AI VIDEO SHOTS $\to$ DRAGNET-STYLE NARRATION $\to$ MUSIC $\to$ SFX $\to$ 200 SECOND FINAL VIDEO."*

**Refactor Compliance:** `CC-04.01 v0.08.00` strictly implements this exact pipeline. The 160-second and 200-second master models decompose the narrative into modular 10-second acts, combining static comic panel crops, 2.5D camera motion, short 5s AI video inserts, spoken narration, and soundscapes.

### 5.2 Studio Media Stack and Workstation Allocation (Blueprint v22.2 §28)
- **Primary Live Production:** OBS Studio (handles scene switching, media playback source, and program recording in MKV).
- **Primary Audio Workstation:** REAPER (records voice tracks, multitrack mix, stems, and loudness processing).
- **Primary Desktop Video Editing:** CapCut Desktop (handles timeline assembly, keyframed pan & scan, transitions, color grading, and subtitle burning).
- **Primary AI Video Generators:** Multi-provider approach (Kling AI, Luma Dream Machine, MiniMax Hailuo, Leonardo Motion, Hedra).

**Refactor Compliance:** CC-04.01 §23, §24, and §32 explicitly assign roles matching these software boundaries, providing CapCut project specifications and OBS media playback integration runbooks without attempting to automate external desktop software.

### 5.3 Episode Architecture and Podcast Placement (Blueprint v22.2 §29)
The Foundation Phase pilot running order is:
1. Jingle & Introduction (devotional quote)
2. Health & Safety Lesson
3. Teaser / Extract
4. Main Podcast Discussion
5. **Approximately 200-second Chronicles of Cupid Black presentation (comic images, transitions, narration, selective animation)**
6. Review & Viewer Invitation
7. Closing Remarks & Community Feature

**Refactor Compliance:** CC-04.01 §11 and §24 explicitly define the relationship between the standalone daily 160s chronicle and the ~200s podcast segment (Step 5 of the Foundation Phase episode), providing clean pre-roll, post-roll, and audio routing controls that prevent live host interruptions during the terminal challenge.

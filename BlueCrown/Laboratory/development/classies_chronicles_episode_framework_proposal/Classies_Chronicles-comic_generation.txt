## Recommended structure: 5 pages per chronicle

| Page | Purpose                 | Content                                                                                    |
| ---- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Opening                 | Establish the setting, characters, time and day's situation.                               |
| 2    | Development             | Introduce the day's activities, conversations and emerging events.                         |
| 3    | Main storyline          | Depict the principal event, discovery, mission or conflict.                                |
| 4    | Resolution / Reflection | Show consequences, character reactions, Bible study or educational lessons where relevant. |
| 5    | Audience challenge      | Present the concluding scene and the day's challenge.                                      |

### Visual dimensions

Each page:

* Aspect ratio: 9:16.
* Orientation: Portrait.
* Resolution: Ideally 1080 × 1920 pixels.
* Layout: Multiple comic panels per page.
* Dialogue: Speech bubbles and captions.
* Continuity: Consistent character appearances, clothing and environments.

## An important production recommendation

I would establish the following standard:

* **3 pages:** Short or relatively uneventful chronicle.
* **5 pages:** Standard chronicle.
* **7–8 pages:** Significant mission, historical exploration or major storyline development.

The audience challenge should always occupy the final page, regardless of the total number of pages.

### One additional architectural consideration

I recommend generating the comic pages sequentially rather than asking an image-generation system to create all five independently without visual references.

Maintain a **Character & Scene Reference Sheet** for each chronicle. This will help preserve:

* Character identity and appearance.
* Clothing and accessories.
* Locations.
* Lighting and atmosphere.
* Scene-to-scene continuity.

**My recommendation for the Classies Chronicles framework: adopt five 9:16 comic pages per daily chronicle as the default production standard, with flexibility to expand to eight pages when the narrative warrants it.**

| Chronicle type | Comic pages | Approx. panels |
|---|---:|---:|
| Light or transitional day | 6 | 18–30 |
| **Standard daily chronicle** | **8** | **24–40** |

A standard page-based comic commonly uses 3–6 panels per page, while vertical webcomic layouts often use 3–5 panels per tall page; keeping panels large and vertically stacked preserves legibility on phones. [gootaku](https://gootaku.com/blog/how-many-panels-should-a-comic-page-have)

## Why 8 pages fits a chronicle

Each chronicle is designed for roughly 3–5 minutes of reading. A short-to-standard vertical comic episode commonly uses about 20–40 panels, while fuller narrative episodes may extend toward 40–80 panels; 8 pages at 3–5 panels per page places a Classies Chronicle in the clear, readable middle of that range. [llamagen](https://llamagen.ai/blogs/how-many-panels-in-a-webtoon-episode)

It also gives enough space for the required daily components:

- Opening scene and featured characters
- Bible-study or Verse of the Day connection
- Health & Safety integration
- Headline or Edubeam development
- Koware role and organizational moment
- Central story advancement
- Final challenge and “solution next time” ending

## Suggested 8-page structure

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Establish setting, time, and featured character(s) |
| 2 | Introduce the day’s central situation or mission |
| 3 | Bible study / Verse of the Day narrative connection |
| 4 | Health & Safety or practical lesson integration |
| 5 | Headline, research, or Edubeam development |
| 6 | Koware role, organizational response, or character decision |
| 7 | Major complication, discovery, or turning point |
| 8 | Resolution beat and the day’s challenge |

The final panel of page 8 should always present the **Daily Challenge**, not its solution. The solution belongs in the following day’s chronicle, preserving the challenge-first release rule. [llamagen](https://llamagen.ai/blogs/how-many-panels-in-a-webtoon-episode)

## Production rule

Use **one clear story beat per panel or screen**, with generous vertical spacing between panels. Do not force a 9:16 page to hold more than about five panels; if a scene needs more, add a page rather than shrinking the artwork or text. [comicink](https://www.comicink.ai/blog/how-to-make-a-webtoon)

So, for the framework, adopt:

> **Default: 8 pages per chronicle.**  
> **Minimum: 6 pages.**  
> **Maximum without special approval: 10 pages.**  
> **Each page: 9:16 portrait, 3–5 panels, ending with the challenge.**

# 1. EXECUTIVE DETERMINATION: ARE THE COMPANION FILES STILL NECESSARY?

## 1.1 Definitive Architectural Finding
**YES. All three CC-02 companion documents remain strictly necessary, but their operational roles have specialized.**

The development of the constitutional `CC-01.01 Unified Production Master Prompt v0.08.00` does not obviate or diminish the necessity of the CC-02 suite. On the contrary, the v0.08.00 master prompt deliberately establishes an architectural division of responsibilities:
1. **`CC-01.01` (Governing Master Prompt):** Acts as the **constitutional authority** for the franchise. It defines the overarching creative mission, world canon, three-clock temporal physics, five-chapter annual calendar hierarchy, four-level permission models, six-stage synthesis principles, and cross-cutting quality gates (`Q01–Q16`, `C01–C22`, `M01–M30`).
2. **`CC-02.01` (Daily Chronicle Generation Protocol):** Acts as the **operational execution runbook**. It details the procedural sequence for executing exactly one daily chronicle run, specifying the eight-phase workflow, preflight source acquisition checklists, Go / No-Go decision gates, deterministic calendar calculations, narrative drafting guidelines, Output B1–B8 generation, Q01–Q16 validation execution, and blocked-run reporting.
3. **`CC-02.02` (Episode Continuity Ledger & Production Handover):** Acts as the **continuity and data schema specification**. It defines the nineteen canonical logical record families (and legacy aliases), the append-only event sourcing architecture, dynamic state projections, strict challenge answer key vault isolation, derivative dependency invalidation, and the fifteen-part portable B6 handover specification required for stateless cross-provider resumption.
4. **`CC-02.03` (Daily Protocol & Ledger Alignment Review):** Acts as the **governance, compatibility, and traceability record**. It documents the architectural migration from v0.04.00 to v0.08.00, cross-platform recommendation adjudications, compatibility matrices, regression test plans, and open governance decision registers.

## 1.2 The Failure Modes of Monolithic Consolidation
Attempting to merge CC-02.01, CC-02.02, and CC-02.03 into CC-01.01 would introduce severe architectural and operational defects:
* **Context Dilution and Instruction Drift:** A consolidated document would exceed 3,500 lines / 35,000 words. Large language models experience attention degradation, instruction skipping, and rule conflation when forced to process massive monolithic prompts.
* **Conflation of Policy, Procedure, and Schema:** CC-01.01 governs *what* the world is and *what* standards must be met; CC-02.01 governs *how* an operator executes a single daily run; CC-02.02 governs *where and how* durable data is structured. Blending these concerns compromises maintainability.
* **Symmetry with Derivative Suites:** The framework maintains a clean, modular architecture across document suites:
  * CC-01 Suite: Master Prompt (`01.01`), QA Manifest (`01.02`), Traceability Log (`01.03`).
  * CC-02 Suite: Operational Runbook (`02.01`), Continuity Schema (`02.02`), Alignment Review (`02.03`).
  * CC-03 Suite: Comic Master (`03.01`), Comic Alignment Review (`03.02`).
  * CC-04 Suite: Media Master (`04.01`), Media Alignment Review (`04.02`).

## 1.3 Refactoring Mandate: Specialization Over Duplication
While the files remain necessary, their contents in v0.04.00 contained redundant restatements of master policy. Under v0.08.00:
* **`CC-02.01`** removes duplicated philosophical treatises, canon summaries, and calendar definitions, focusing purely on operational execution, preflight checks, Go/Blocked logic, and phase sequencing.
* **`CC-02.02`** removes narrative writing instructions, focusing purely on logical record schemas, event sourcing mechanics, access partitions, and the stateless B6 handover contract.
* **`CC-02.03`** focuses on cross-document alignment, platform consultation synthesis, regression testing, and controlled adoption tracking.

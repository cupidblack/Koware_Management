## 1. Executive determination

The recommended outcome is a single top-level **Unified Episode
Framework** that coordinates the Classies Chronicles
season/chapter/episode/chronicle hierarchy and daily production while
preserving the specialist responsibilities of the existing v0.04.00
documents.

The framework is not a replacement for the Unified Master Prompt, Daily
Chronicle Generation Protocol, Episode Continuity Ledger, Master Comic
Generation Prompt or Master Media Generation Prompt. It provides their
shared orchestration rules and points to the specialist contracts for
their detailed execution.

The central architecture is:

-   **Approved canon and governance** define what is established and
    permitted.
-   **The public chronicle** is the literary story for one authorized
    day.
-   **The episode archive** records the evidence, fictional events,
    mission state, decisions and handovers.
-   **Specialist prompts** govern daily execution, continuity records,
    comics and audiovisual production.
-   **The authorized human editor** approves, locks, promotes, releases
    and publishes.

The resulting v0.07.00 document is a complete proposed prompt, not a
claim that the project's operating calendar, storage schema, consent
system, secure answer vault or production tools have been deployed or
approved.

### Specific Jules recommendation corrections

The detailed Jules proposal contains useful creative and operational
ideas but also several recommendations that would exceed the authority
of the supplied sources if adopted literally:

-   It proposes specific annual hiatus intervals. These are not
    established by the original five-chapter proposal and are not
    adopted as approved calendar facts.
-   It treats some Edubeam mechanics as fixed operational laws. The
    v0.04.00 master identifies several such mechanics as proposed, so
    they remain subject to canon approval.
-   It proposes a mandatory three-part output including a restricted
    answer. The current v0.04.00 master allows the answer to remain
    uncreated where secure custody is unavailable; therefore C is
    conditional, not mandatory.
-   It treats some physical archive paths and schemas as deterministic.
    The continuity ledger defines logical record families but leaves
    physical implementation to the approved Studio Blueprint.
-   It proposes mandatory daily routines, briefings, recalibrations and
    mission returns. The refactor retains the dramatic rhythm as
    flexible guidance, subject to accepted story state.

ownership:

1.  Production timezone, effective annual calendar, phase boundaries and
    gap policy.
2.  Official `phase_index` to chapter/legacy-ID crosswalk.
3.  Physical archive structure and write permissions under the approved
    Studio Blueprint.
4.  Eternal Dreamfall's date-to-week anchor and daily passage selection.
5.  Current aliased role chart, role-check semantics and permitted role
    spotlight cap.
6.  Individual character canon status and real-person portrayal/diary
    permissions.
7.  Edubeam capabilities by era and character.
8.  Restricted challenge-answer custody and release policy.
9.  Episode compilation, title approval and chapter/season roll-up
    rules.
10. Comic visual identity, page/collage standards and asset approval.
11. Media runtime, provider access, credit budgets, voice and music
    rights.
12. Service Cards, 43 Clues, QR/Buzz Code, Palmier/myCred and rewards.
13. Authorized repository destination, branch, revision control and
    publication workflow.

A missing decision should be marked `AWAITING_EDITORIAL_DECISION`, not
filled with an invented value.

1.  Ratify or reject the framework and identify its effective date and
    scope.
2.  Verify the current approved Studio Blueprint and physical archive
    layout.
3.  Approve the production timezone, effective calendar and legacy ID
    crosswalk.
4.  Approve the Eternal Dreamfall date/week mapping.
5.  Confirm role chart and character canon/rights rules.
6.  Confirm Edubeam era limits and mission-state vocabulary.
7.  Confirm the nineteen logical record families and physical
    compatibility mapping.
8.  Establish actual restricted challenge-answer custody and release
    policy.
9.  Run read-only preflight and negative tests.
10. Conduct one authorized daily pilot with separate A/B and no
    unauthorized C.
11. Test any authorized write with revision/concurrency protection.
12. After ten accepted chronicles, test a human-led episode compilation.
13. Adopt the specialist comic/media workflow only through their own
    approval and pilot gates.

documents, prior platform compilations and earlier candidate versions
for traceability. Do not overwrite or backdate historical records.

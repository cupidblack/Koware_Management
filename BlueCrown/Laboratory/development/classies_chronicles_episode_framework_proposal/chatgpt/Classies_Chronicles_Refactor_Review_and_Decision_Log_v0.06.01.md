## 7.3 Unit-level arithmetic observations

- `40 + 40 + 40 = 120 seconds`; `40 + 40 + 48 = 128 seconds`, not 160. A valid *illustrative* three-scene 160-second allocation is `(7×8) + (7×8) + (6×8) = 56 + 56 + 48 = 160 seconds`.
- `16 × 10 sec = 160 sec`; `20 × 10 sec = 200 sec`.
- `10 × 160 sec = 1,600 sec = 26 min 40 sec`, so an exact 20-minute final episode requires reduction or a different original format.
- For 9:16 portrait cells arranged `c` columns and `r` rows, outer `width : height = 9c : 16r`. Thus 2×2→18:32 = 9:16; 2×4→18:64 = 9:32; 1×8→9:128. The outer aspect does not guarantee readable text; actual per-cell resolution must be tested.

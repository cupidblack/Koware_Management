# Classies Chronicles — Framework Review and Refactored Production Architecture

**Recommended revision:** v0.01.07
**Assessment date:** Thursday, 29 October 2026
**Project:** Season 00 — Foundation Phase
**Primary episode:** S00E01 — The Signal Before Sunrise
**Status:** Refactored Production Candidate — Pending Approval

I have reviewed the supplied materials, including the original framework, the v0.01.05 proposal, the GitHub review, the Thursday 29 October episode, and all four proposed graphics.

I have also incorporated the previously established Classies Chronicles architecture, including the five-year Eternal Dreamfall Bible Study, Koware Group's organisational structure, the Millennium Timezone, EduBeam, the historical and future timelines, service cards, and the wider Buzzjuice ecosystem.

My recommendation is to retain the original concept but substantially improve its narrative architecture and production methodology.

There is one particularly important development: **The Signal Before Sunrise should no longer be treated as simply a story about a team travelling through time. It should become a story about responsibility, discovery, uncertainty, and the consequences of technology.**

---

# PART 1 — REVIEW AND FINDINGS

## 1.1 What the original proposal gets right

The v0.01.05 proposal contains several valuable elements that should remain.

| Component                     | Assessment                             | Recommendation     |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Ten-working-day structure     | Strong                                 | Retain             |
| Two Monday–Friday workweeks   | Strong                                 | Retain             |
| 4:00 a.m. morning routine     | Good character development             | Retain             |
| Eternal Dreamfall Bible Study | Essential                              | Retain             |
| YouVersion Verse of the Day   | Essential                              | Retain             |
| Health & Safety integration   | Valuable educational component         | Strengthen         |
| Current news                  | Important contextual component         | Expand             |
| Historical news               | Interesting historical dimension       | Retain             |
| Future news                   | Important science-fiction dimension    | Formalise          |
| Koware Group operations       | Strong worldbuilding                   | Retain             |
| EduBeam missions              | Central to the universe                | Strengthen         |
| Service cards                 | Commercial and educational opportunity | Formalise          |
| Friday reporting              | Good corporate structure               | Retain             |
| Weekend activities            | Useful character development           | Retain selectively |

The main weakness is that the original proposal sometimes describes what characters do without explaining why those activities matter to the larger episode.

For example, repeatedly showing the laboratory specialist adjusting EduBeam equipment is not necessarily interesting.

However, showing the specialist adjusting the equipment because an unexplained signal has appeared, and because the team might not be able to return safely, creates a compelling story.

That distinction should guide the entire production framework.

## 1.2 The most important recommendation from GitHub

The GitHub response identifies the strongest narrative development already present in the archive:

**The appearance of the message:**

> DO NOT RETURN.

This is considerably stronger than introducing another unrelated adventure.

It provides a central mystery that can sustain an entire ten-working-day episode.

The following developments should therefore become the principal narrative foundations:

1. The 9 July 2026 Singapore EduBeam event is an important historical continuity anchor.
2. Enka remains the primary character in the foundation storyline.
3. Cu/Q remains an alias associated with later Kuala Lumpur continuity.
4. The Thursday 29 October 2026 episode establishes the unexplained warning.
5. The warning becomes the central unresolved mystery.
6. The story investigates whether the warning is genuine, corrupted, deliberately transmitted, or generated by another temporal phenomenon.
7. The team prioritises safety over completing its assignment.

I agree with these recommendations.

One important distinction: the warning must not be explained prematurely. Its origin, purpose and authenticity should remain unresolved until the appropriate subsequent storyline.

# PART 2 — UPDATED NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE

## 2.1 The identity of Classies Chronicles

The project should operate as a combination of:

* Fictional storytelling.
* Personal chronicles.
* Historical exploration.
* Contemporary news awareness.
* Biblical reflection.
* Health and Safety education.
* Corporate research and development.
* Science fiction.
* Practical life lessons.
* Community engagement.
* Interactive entertainment.

The result should be an interconnected storytelling universe rather than a conventional television series.

The audience should be able to follow the characters, learn something useful, discover new places, understand real-world developments and participate in the activities emerging from the stories.

## 2.2 Narrative hierarchy

The following structure is recommended.

| Level               | Structure                     | Purpose                                   |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Universe            | Classies Chronicles           | Entire fictional and documentary universe |
| Season              | Approximately 2 months        | Major storyline period                    |
| Phase               | Approximately 2 weeks         | Temporal or thematic movement             |
| Episode             | 10 working days               | Main narrative assignment                 |
| Daily Chronicle     | Individual working day        | Daily story progression                   |
| Scene               | Individual narrative sequence | Character and plot development            |
| Interactive content | Cards, challenges, quizzes    | Audience participation                    |

This preserves the established five-season annual structure.

Each season contains four approximately two-week phases:

1. Present → Future.
2. Return to Present.
3. Present → Past.
4. Return to Present.

The original five-season arrangement remains the annual planning baseline.

The ten-working-day episode is the production unit, not a requirement that every story must take place across exactly ten fictional days.

**Important distinction:** Production time and fictional time are independent.

A ten-working-day production can describe events spanning several hours, days, months or even years.

This allows the narrative to remain flexible.

# PART 3 — CANONICAL CONTINUITY

## 3.1 Principal character

**Enka remains the central identity of the Foundation Phase.**

The story should follow his experiences, responsibilities, decisions, reflections and relationship with Koware Group.

Cu/Q should not replace Enka as the protagonist.

The alias belongs to the later Kuala Lumpur continuity and can be introduced when the appropriate storyline requires it.

## 3.2 Principal locations

| Location               | Narrative responsibility                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Adenta, Accra          | Enka's home and personal environment                   |
| Amrahia, Accra         | Koware Group headquarters and R&D operations           |
| Las Vegas, Nevada      | Millennium Timezone historical reference               |
| Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia | Principal temporal research destination                |
| Singapore              | Important historical EduBeam continuity                |
| Other locations        | Additional historical, present-day and future missions |

These locations should retain their distinct identities.

Las Vegas is not automatically the teleportation destination.

Kuala Lumpur is not automatically the historical reference.

The actual Millennium Timezone outputs determine the temporal coordinates.

## 3.3 The Millennium Timezone

The system should remain the authoritative source of temporal coordinates.

The established endpoints are:

* Historical: `?endpoint=historical`
* Probe: `?endpoint=probe`

Reference:

[Millennium Timezone](https://buzzjuice.net/shared/millennium-timezone/millennium-timezone.php?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

A particularly important historical example must remain preserved:

`2167-04-30T01:13:07Z`

This previously established probe timestamp should not be silently replaced with a conventional date.

All future timestamps must be treated according to the actual system output.

If the system returns an unusual date, the story should investigate its implications rather than correcting it for convenience.

## 3.4 EduBeam operating limitations

EduBeam should have meaningful limitations.

The established operating principles remain:

* Four personnel are selected for a mission.
* The team is charged with EduBeam particles.
* Mission duration depends on available charge.
* Blue Crown communications provide the connection to headquarters.
* The R&D specialist monitors the mission.
* Materialisation of objects is possible.
* Communications may fail.
* Environmental conditions may interfere with operations.
* Equipment failure may prevent normal return.
* Natural recharge and emergency recovery are difficult.

The possibility of remaining stranded for years should remain a genuine narrative risk.

However, emergency procedures should be established before deployment rather than introduced conveniently when something goes wrong.

# PART 4 — THE NEW TEN-WORKING-DAY EPISODE

## S00E01 — THE SIGNAL BEFORE SUNRISE

**Central question:**

*What happens when a signal appears to be genuine, but nobody knows whether following it will save the team or put them in greater danger?*

### WEEK ONE — DISCOVERY AND VERIFICATION

The first week establishes the mystery, the technology, the characters and the operational consequences.

| Day         | Narrative title     | Primary development                                   | Intended outcome                          |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Monday 1    | Before the Signal   | Enka's morning, initial anomaly and mission context   | Establish the mystery                     |
| Tuesday 1   | Verification        | Examination of telemetry and incomplete transmissions | Establish safety procedures               |
| Wednesday 1 | Beyond the Signal   | Focus on the deployed team                            | Introduce physical consequences           |
| Thursday 1  | Three Timelines     | Historical, present and future comparisons            | Establish the temporal mystery            |
| Friday 1    | The Neighbours Know | Community concerns and operational accountability     | Expand the conflict beyond the laboratory |

### MONDAY 1 — BEFORE THE SIGNAL

**Opening sequence**

The camera begins outside Enka's residence.

It captures the surrounding environment, garden, neighbouring houses and the transition from night to morning.

At 4:00 a.m., the alarm sounds.

Enka wakes and activates the Eternal Dreamfall Bible Study.

The day's reading establishes the spiritual foundation.

He subsequently reads the YouVersion Verse of the Day.

The morning continues with domestic responsibilities, fitness, breakfast and preparation for work.

At approximately 6:30 a.m., he connects with the day's team.

During the commute:

* Capture Accra's morning activities.
* Introduce relevant current news.
* Introduce meaningful human interactions.
* Integrate a service card where appropriate.
* Establish the day's central theme.

At Amrahia, the R&D team discovers an unexplained signal discrepancy.

The signal does not yet contain enough information to identify its origin.

**Closing question:**

Why is the system receiving a transmission that nobody initiated?

### TUESDAY 1 — VERIFICATION

The laboratory investigates the anomaly.

The R&D specialist examines:

* Equipment logs.
* Transmission records.
* Environmental readings.
* Return-channel activity.
* Communication timestamps.

Enka establishes the operational rule:

An incomplete instruction cannot authorise a safety-critical action.

The deployed team receives updated information.

Meanwhile, neighbours begin noticing unusual activity around the headquarters.

**Closing question:**

Is the signal coming from the deployed team, the laboratory, or somewhere else?

### WEDNESDAY 1 — BEYOND THE SIGNAL

The story shifts towards the four-person deployed team.

The audience experiences the probe environment.

Introduce the team's individual responsibilities and personalities.

The team encounters an environmental or operational challenge.

A communication interruption makes it difficult to distinguish between:

* An ordinary technical failure.
* A delayed instruction.
* An external transmission.
* A potentially dangerous anomaly.

The team must decide whether to continue or stop.

The decision should be influenced by the week's biblical and safety themes.

**Closing question:**

What happens when the team can no longer trust the information arriving through its communications equipment?

### THURSDAY 1 — THREE TIMELINES

The story returns to headquarters.

Enka and the R&D team compare:

1. The historical Las Vegas reference.
2. Contemporary Accra.
3. The Kuala Lumpur probing destination.

Current news must remain distinguishable from historical reporting and fictional future scenarios.

This episode should explore how similar problems can emerge in different periods.

The team begins to question whether the temporal coordinates themselves are contributing to the anomaly.

**Closing question:**

Are the three timelines independent, or are they connected by something the system has not identified?

### FRIDAY 1 — THE NEIGHBOURS KNOW

The laboratory's unusual activities begin attracting greater attention.

Neighbours ask questions.

Some are curious. Others are concerned.

The liaison personnel must balance confidentiality with community responsibility.

Meanwhile, management reviews the mission's progress.

The team remains deployed.

The first week concludes with a technical discovery that makes the second week necessary.

**Closing question:**

What happens when the consequences of a private experiment begin affecting people outside the organisation?

---

## WEEK TWO — ESCALATION AND ACCOUNTABILITY

The second week should deepen the mystery and force the organisation to make difficult decisions.

| Day         | Narrative title          | Primary development                 | Intended outcome             |
| ----------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Monday 2    | The Warning Returns      | Signal becomes clearer              | Escalate the mystery         |
| Tuesday 2   | A Divided Team           | Disagreement about proceeding       | Introduce human conflict     |
| Wednesday 2 | The Singapore Connection | Revisit 9 July 2026                 | Establish continuity         |
| Thursday 2  | The Return Decision      | Review mission recovery options     | Force a leadership decision  |
| Friday 2    | Do Not Return            | Final warning and unresolved ending | Bridge into the next episode |

### MONDAY 2 — THE WARNING RETURNS

The signal returns.

Its contents are clearer, but its origin remains uncertain.

The laboratory compares the new transmission against previous records.

Enka requests a comprehensive verification exercise.

The return procedure is reviewed.

The team is informed of the situation without presenting unverified information as established fact.

**Closing question:**

If the warning is genuine, what danger is waiting on the return route?

### TUESDAY 2 — A DIVIDED TEAM

The mission team develops internal disagreements.

One member believes the assignment should continue.

Another believes the equipment should be placed on hold.

Others are concerned about the consequences of remaining in the probe zone.

This is an opportunity to introduce believable personalities and differences in judgement.

The disagreement must not make one character automatically foolish and another automatically correct.

The audience should understand the reasoning behind each position.

**Closing question:**

Who has the authority to decide when the available evidence is insufficient?

### WEDNESDAY 2 — THE SINGAPORE CONNECTION

The R&D department revisits the 9 July 2026 Singapore EduBeam event.

Previously recorded information is examined.

The team compares:

* Earlier equipment behaviour.
* Communication anomalies.
* Materialisation records.
* Temporal discrepancies.
* The current warning.

The Singapore event should not be rewritten or replaced.

It is part of the established history.

The objective is to determine whether the current anomaly resembles something that occurred previously.

**Closing question:**

Was the Singapore incident an isolated event, or was it the beginning of something larger?

### THURSDAY 2 — THE RETURN DECISION

The organisation must evaluate the available evidence.

The Corporate Manager, R&D personnel and mission team review the possible outcomes.

The options include:

* Continue the assignment.
* Suspend the research.
* Attempt a controlled return.
* Wait for further communication.
* Initiate an emergency recovery procedure.

The decision must respect the established EduBeam limitations.

Enka must demonstrate leadership.

The correct response is not necessarily the quickest response.

**Closing question:**

Can the organisation safely bring its people home when it cannot fully establish what is happening to the return channel?

### FRIDAY 2 — DO NOT RETURN

The final day begins with the normal morning routine.

The organisation prepares its final operational briefing.

The R&D specialist reviews the signal.

Another transmission arrives.

The message is clearer.

**DO NOT RETURN.**

The laboratory attempts to identify its origin.

The system cannot provide a reliable answer.

The episode ends without explaining who sent the warning.

The team remains at the centre of an unresolved situation.

The next episode investigates:

* Who sent the message?
* Why was it transmitted?
* Is it connected to Singapore?
* Is the return channel compromised?
* Does the message originate from the future?
* Is somebody attempting to prevent the team from returning?

**Final narrative principle:**

The episode should conclude with an unresolved mystery, not an unresolved production process.

The audience should feel that the story has reached a meaningful milestone even though the larger mystery remains open.

# PART 5 — THE DAILY CONTENT GENERATION ENGINE

This is one of the most important changes.

The original proposal contains several reading and research stages. These should be consolidated into a controlled editorial pipeline.

## 5.1 The six-stage Eternal Dreamfall pipeline

Every daily Chronicle should follow this process.

| Stage | Name                        | Source                              | Output                     |
| ----- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1     | Eternal Dreamfall Motif     | Five-year Bible plan                | Spiritual reflection       |
| 2     | Eternal Dreamfall Chronicle | YouVersion                          | Updated reflection         |
| 3     | Refreshed Eternal Dreamfall | Buzzjuice H&S                       | Practical application      |
| 4     | Headlined Eternal Dreamfall | Historical, current and future news | News-integrated reflection |
| 5     | Classies Eternal Dreamfall  | Daily Diary and archives            | Personal narrative         |
| 6     | Classies Chronicles         | Koware organisational records       | Final daily story          |

These are production stages, not six independently published stories.

The audience should receive one coherent narrative.

The intermediate materials should remain available in the production archive.

### Stage 1 — Bible reading

Source:

[Eternal Dreamfall Five-Year Bible Reading Plan](https://github.com/cupidblack/Classies_Chronicles/blob/main/Eternal_Dreamfall_Bible_Study/list_of_biblical_texts-5_year_reading_plan-1.txt?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The reading plan uses Sunday as the first day of the week and begins from the first full week of 2025.

The assigned passages must be identified using the actual date.

### Stage 2 — YouVersion

Source:

[YouVersion Verse of the Day](https://www.bible.com/verse-of-the-day?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The Verse of the Day should deepen the existing Bible-study motif.

It must not replace the assigned reading.

### Stage 3 — Health & Safety

Source:

[Buzzjuice Health & Safety Courses](https://github.com/cupidblack/Buzzjuice_Courses/tree/main/Health%20%26%20Safety?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The H&S lesson should emerge naturally from the biblical reflection.

Examples include:

* Workplace safety.
* Electrical safety.
* Emergency preparedness.
* Personal wellbeing.
* Road safety.
* Communication failures.
* Environmental hazards.
* Mental alertness.
* Risk assessment.

Specific course titles should only be attributed when verified against the actual course repository.

### Stage 4 — News integration

Three separate news collections must be maintained.

**Historical news**

News relevant to the Millennium Timezone historical reference.

**Current news**

News published on the actual publication date.

**Probing news**

News associated with the probing location and fictional temporal scenario.

An important existing rule must be retained:

Current news always refers to the actual publication date, regardless of the fictional period in which the story takes place.

Future news must be explicitly identified as fictional scenarios rather than presented as verified journalism.

The historical and probing news collections should not be confused with one another.

### Stage 5 — Personal continuity

The latest Daily Diary entries should be reviewed.

Relevant personal events, experiences, reflections and observations should influence the story.

The diary is particularly important because it connects the fictional narrative with real experiences.

For example, an observation made by Enka in Accra might inspire an analogous experience for the deployed team in Kuala Lumpur.

However, an analogy must not be presented as a literal historical event.

### Stage 6 — Final narrative

The final story incorporates:

* Bible-study reflections.
* Current news.
* Historical context.
* Future scenarios.
* Personal experiences.
* Koware operations.
* Character development.
* EduBeam research.
* Community activities.
* Service cards.
* The central episode mystery.

The result is the daily Classies Chronicle.

# PART 6 — DAILY PRODUCTION FORMAT

The daily routine should remain recognisable without becoming repetitive.

## Standard morning sequence

| Time        | Activity                                                            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 04:00       | Alarm and Eternal Dreamfall Bible reading                           |
| 04:30       | Domestic management and preparation                                 |
| 05:00       | Second alarm, Verse of the Day and reflection                       |
| 05:00–06:00 | Fitness or rest, depending on the day                               |
| 06:00       | Freshening up and breakfast preparation                             |
| 06:30       | Team connections and commute                                        |
| 07:00       | Adenta base reporting for the designated national service personnel |
| 08:00–08:59 | Workstation preparation and news review                             |
| 09:00       | Scheduled corporate briefing where applicable                       |

The fitness schedule should remain flexible.

The original Tuesday and Saturday fitness preference, with occasional Thursday sessions, should be preserved.

Not every day needs to show every activity in detail.

The morning routine should provide continuity, atmosphere and character development.

## Corporate briefing schedule

The following arrangement should be used as the production baseline:

| Day       | All-staff briefing |
| --------- | ------------------ |
| Monday    | Yes                |
| Tuesday   | No                 |
| Wednesday | No                 |
| Thursday  | Yes                |
| Friday    | Yes                |

The second Monday briefing reviews the previous week's findings.

The second Thursday briefing concentrates on return preparations.

The second Friday briefing reviews the completed mission and integrates the findings into Koware's operations.

## Friday afternoon

The Friday closing routine should preserve the original corporate culture.

Activities include:

* Mission reports.
* Review of findings.
* Action plans.
* Equipment checks.
* Security checks.
* Weekend preparations.
* Team prayers and thanksgiving.
* Member activities.
* Club and group events.

The next EduBeam team should already have been selected before the current team departs.

The selection process should remain transparent enough to support accountability while preserving confidential operational information.

# PART 7 — CHARACTER AND ORGANISATIONAL MANAGEMENT

The story should use Koware Group's organisational structure as its authoritative reference.

Characters should not be introduced simply because a scene needs another person.

Every recurring character should have:

1. An established identity.
2. An organisational role.
3. A narrative responsibility.
4. Relevant relationships.
5. A continuity record.

The four-person EduBeam team should have differentiated responsibilities.

For example, its operational functions might include:

* Mission coordination.
* Environmental research.
* Technical investigation.
* Community engagement and documentation.

These are functional assignments, not new canonical character identities.

The actual names and aliases should be retrieved from the organisational chart before final scripts are produced.

The R&D specialist should remain particularly important.

This character connects the deployed team to headquarters and becomes the audience's primary window into the technical limitations of EduBeam.

# PART 8 — SERVICE CARDS AND COMMERCIAL INTEGRATION

I recommend retaining the existing service-card concept as a permanent feature of Classies Chronicles.

Each episode should generate approximately **four to five service cards**.

Each card must contain a genuinely useful life hack demonstrated within the story.

## Standard service-card structure

| Component           | Description                                                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Card title          | Short, memorable title                                                   |
| Life hack           | Practical information                                                    |
| Story connection    | Scene where the lesson is demonstrated                                   |
| Category            | Work, home, road, money, mind, technology, people, safety or environment |
| Character           | Character associated with the lesson                                     |
| Buzz Code           | Unique seven-character code                                              |
| QR activation       | Link to the relevant Buzzjuice destination                               |
| Profile association | Personal collection or achievement                                       |

The cards should not feel like advertisements inserted into a story.

They should emerge naturally from the characters' experiences.

### Commercial integration

Products and services can appear naturally in the narrative.

The established format should remain:

**Story → Problem → Product appears → Quick demonstration → Story continues → Optional CTA.**

At the end of the episode, publish a compilation of featured products with a link to the dedicated episode-products page.

This provides an opportunity to connect Classies Chronicles with:

* WooCommerce.
* Affiliate marketing.
* Buzzjuice subscriptions.
* Challenges.
* Learning activities.
* Community engagement.
* Points and XP.
* Badges and leaderboards.

The wider engagement loop becomes:

**Episode → Prediction → Reveal → Challenge → Discussion → Next Episode.**

This turns every episode into a reusable interactive learning and entertainment resource.

## 9.2 The contemporary identity

The aerial campus image introduces a different visual direction.

# PART 11 — DAILY PRODUCTION QUALITY CONTROL

Before publishing a daily Chronicle, the following checks should be performed.

| Check                    | Requirement                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Date                     | Correct publication date              |
| Bible reading            | Correct five-year plan assignment     |
| Verse of the Day         | Verified against YouVersion           |
| H&S                      | Relevant practical lesson             |
| Historical news          | Correct historical date               |
| Current news             | Actual publication-day reporting      |
| Probing news             | Clearly distinguished from fiction    |
| Diary                    | Relevant personal continuity          |
| Characters               | Correct identities and aliases        |
| Organisational structure | Consistent with Koware records        |
| Timezone                 | Actual system output                  |
| EduBeam                  | Established operating limitations     |
| Narrative                | Advances the main storyline           |
| Service cards            | Four to five useful cards per episode |
| Products                 | Natural integration                   |
| References               | Verifiable sources                    |
| Publication              | Text, image and supporting assets     |

### Important editorial safeguards

Four distinctions must always be maintained:

**1. Historical facts versus fictional events**

A fictional character may interact with a real historical setting, but invented events must not be presented as verified history.

**2. Current news versus future scenarios**

Current news remains anchored to its actual publication date.

Future reporting must be labelled fictional.

**3. Established canon versus proposed developments**

A suggestion in a previous AI-generated outline does not automatically become canonical.

**4. Verified technical outputs versus narrative inventions**

Actual Millennium Timezone outputs must be preserved.

A fictional signal, equipment failure or anomaly must be identified as a narrative development rather than an actual system output.

The framework should support multiple formats without requiring every daily Chronicle to become a fully produced multimedia episode.

I recommend a staged approach.

| Format                | Production priority | Purpose                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------- |

| Cover image           | Essential           | Recognition and publication |
| Comic page            | High                | Visual storytelling         |

| Podcast adaptation    | Medium              | Audio storytelling          |
| Interactive quiz      | Medium              | Audience engagement         |
| Service cards         | Essential           | Practical learning          |
| Full animated episode | Later               | Advanced production         |

The current use of CapCut, REAPER, OBS and Voicemeeter Potato provides a suitable foundation for this approach.

For example:


This allows the same scene to be referenced in:

* The written story.
* Comic panels.

* Voice recordings.
* Sound design.
* Service cards.

It also makes future automation significantly easier.

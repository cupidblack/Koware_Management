# 1. Executive Summary

The studio should now be treated as a **small AI-assisted educational media operating system**, rather than simply a podcast recording setup.

Version 20.0 introduces a major architectural clarification:

> **The AI Desk is one persistent studio subsystem, not one AI instance per episode.**

The AI Desk owns the persistent AI operating environment, persona, prompts, policies, tools, indexes and production controls. Episodes remain the source of truth for episode-specific media, context, transcripts, research and production artifacts. The AI Desk references those episode repositories when an episode is selected or queried; it does not create a separate AI Desk for every episode.

The architecture therefore separates:

```text
AI DESK = persistent intelligence / personality / orchestration layer
EPISODE = authoritative content / media / context layer
```

A rebuildable search/index layer may cache references or embeddings for performance, but it is never the canonical source of episode data.

The immediate objective is to produce:

> **Classies Chronicles — Pilot Season — Episode 1 — The Foundation Phase**

while simultaneously commissioning a reusable production system capable of supporting:

- studio podcasts
- remote guests
- WhatsApp / Google Meet / Zoom discussions
- AI co-hosts
- presentations and slides
- AI-generated comics
- animated stories
- AI music
- mobile reporting
- livestreams
- educational content
- community-generated stories
- social-media clips
- Buzzjuice Courses promotion
- subscriptions
- crowdfunding
- future commercial media production

The architecture is deliberately **lean first, scalable second**.

The governing principle is:

> **Do not build the complicated studio before proving the simple studio.**

# 3. Core Architectural Decisions

The following decisions are now definitive for Version 20.0.

### Decision 1 — ENVY is the master broadcast workstation

The HP ENVY m7 remains the central studio control machine.

It should handle:

- OBS
- primary camera
- host presentation
- slides
- media playback
- programme recording
- Pavilion programme feed
- live scene control
- master recording
- final programme monitoring

It should **not** simultaneously carry unnecessary AI generation, heavy browser workloads, virtual machines or intensive rendering.

---

### Decision 2 — Pavilion is the producer / AI / guest workstation

The Pavilion handles:

- Blue Yeti
- remote guests
- producer communication
- AI interaction
- research
- REAPER stem recording
- secondary camera
- backup video
- AI TTS
- guest-call management

The Pavilion's 8 GB RAM is the most significant current workstation bottleneck.

**Recommended upgrade: 16 GB RAM.**

---

### Decision 3 — S24 Ultra is the mobile production unit

The Samsung S24 Ultra should remain primarily independent from the fixed studio.

It becomes:

> **Dynamic Camera / Mobile Production Unit**

Use it for:

- mobile podcasting
- field interviews
- short status posts
- live streams
- meetups
- B-roll
- spontaneous discussions
- vehicle/drive recordings
- alternative camera angles

---

### Decision 4 — OBS is the programme recorder

OBS provides:

> **Master Programme**

---

### Decision 5 — REAPER is the stem recorder

REAPER provides:

> **Independent Pavilion-originating audio where technically possible**

This distinction is important.

OBS preserves the programme.

REAPER preserves recoverable components.

---

### Decision 6 — V8S remains supplemental

The V8S is formally:

> **Stereo Programme Mixer / Secondary Audio Source**

It is not assumed to provide independently addressable microphone channels.

---

### Decision 7 — 44.1 kHz remains the commissioning baseline

Because the existing hardware chain has already been tested successfully at 44.1 kHz, the first complete studio commissioning should remain at:

> **44,100 Hz**

The studio can later test a controlled 48 kHz migration.

For the pilot:

> **stability takes precedence over theoretical optimization.**

---

### Decision 8 — Physical routing comes before virtual routing

Do not introduce:

- Voicemeeter
- VB-Cable
- NDI
- network audio
- complex virtual mixers

until the physical system demonstrates an actual requirement for them.

---

### Decision 9 — AI gets operating modes

AI should not simply be "on" or "off".

The studio uses:

1. **LISTEN**
2. **ASSIST**
3. **CO-HOST**
4. **AUTONOMOUS**

with an independent:

> **AI MUTE**

control.

---

### Decision 10 — Producer always retains emergency control

AI may become conversationally autonomous within the production environment, but the producer must always be able to immediately mute or remove its audio from the programme.

> Blue Yeti

**Backup:**

> Intel Smart Sound Technology Digital Microphone Array

## V8S condenser

Use when:

- speaker can remain approximately 1 inch away
- controlled environment is available
- close-range recording is practical

Do not compensate for distance by excessive gain.

Testing has already demonstrated that this introduces:

- noise
- static
- distortion

---

# 9. Audio Gain Philosophy

The studio should solve weak audio primarily through:

1. microphone placement
2. microphone distance
3. appropriate source gain
4. room control
5. clean routing

rather than aggressive post-recording amplification.

A clean slightly quiet recording is recoverable.

A clipped or heavily distorted recording is not.

# 34. AI Desk Knowledge Architecture

The AI Desk should answer questions using a retrieval hierarchy rather than dumping every episode into every prompt.

```text
QUERY
  ↓
PERSISTENT PERSONA
  ↓
GLOBAL CHRONICLES KNOWLEDGE
  ↓
SELECTED EPISODE
  ↓
EPISODE CONTEXT
  ↓
RELEVANT RETRIEVED PASSAGES
  ↓
AI RESPONSE
```

The AI should not automatically ingest every available episode into every live prompt.

Instead it should retrieve only the information relevant to the current question.

Examples:

> "What happened in S01E01?"

→ retrieve S01E01 transcript/show notes/context.

> "How have we handled this topic before?"

→ search across all approved episodes.

> "How should I respond to this guest?"

→ use the active persona + relationship file + current episode context.

> "What was the headline about flooding?"

→ retrieve the relevant episode-context news record.

# 47. AI Return Path

The current proven system is:

```text
PAVILION → ENVY
```

The desired live system is:

```text
PAVILION ⇄ ENVY
```

The return path is required so the AI can reliably hear the programme participants.

The complete conversational topology must eventually satisfy:

```text
HOST hears GUEST
HOST hears AI

GUEST hears HOST
GUEST hears AI

AI hears HOST
AI hears GUEST

PRODUCER hears HOST
PRODUCER hears GUEST
PRODUCER hears AI
```

with:

> **No feedback loop.**

The physical Pavilion → ENVY path remains unchanged while this is developed.

Virtual routing should be introduced only when an actual return-path requirement has been demonstrated.

# 64. Consent Revocation

When a member disables participation:

1. stop future harvesting
2. exclude the user from future episode processing
3. delete staging copies that no longer have a valid processing basis
4. remove them from active AI-generation datasets
5. prevent future story generation using revoked material
6. preserve only information that must legitimately be retained

The system should distinguish:

> **withdrawal from future processing**

from:

> **removal of previously published content**

Those are different operational events.

```text

 ├── schemas

 ├── documentation

 │
 ├── raw video
 ├── raw audio
 ├── large AI assets
 ├── project files
 └── exports
```

A Git repository may contain episode text files and metadata when practical.

Large media should normally remain in cloud/archive storage unless the repository is deliberately designed for large-file management.

The AI Desk must store stable references:

```text

path

last_verified
```

```text

    ↓

    ↓

```

The episode identity must not depend on a particular storage vendor.

# 127. Definitive Operating Principles

### 1.
**ENVY is the master broadcast workstation.**

### 2.
**Pavilion is the producer / AI / guest workstation.**

### 3.
**S24 Ultra is the mobile production unit.**

### 4.
**OBS preserves the programme.**

### 5.
**REAPER preserves stems.**

### 6.
**The V8S is a supplemental stereo programme mixer, not the multichannel recording foundation.**

### 7.
**The proven Pavilion → Blue Yeti → Daakro → ENVY path is retained.**

### 8.
**44.1 kHz / 24-bit is the initial commissioning standard because it is already proven stable.**

### 9.
**Physical routing comes before virtual routing.**

### 10.
**AI can operate as an increasingly autonomous co-host, but the producer always has immediate mute authority.**

### 11.
**AI personality is versioned and deliberately evolved.**

### 12.
**AI assets are registered and licensing status is tracked.**

### 13.
**Member-generated Chronicles content is consent-driven.**

### 14.
**Raw media is never overwritten.**

### 15.
**Every important recording has a master and recoverable components.**

### 16.
**The pilot optimizes for reliability rather than technical sophistication.**

### 17.
**One primary software platform is selected per production function; alternatives remain available as fallbacks.**

### 18.
**Every new capability is commissioned independently before being combined with the complete system.**

### 19.
**The Foundation Phase is both an episode and a studio prototype.**

### 20.
**Season 1 should only scale after the Foundation Phase proves the production system.**

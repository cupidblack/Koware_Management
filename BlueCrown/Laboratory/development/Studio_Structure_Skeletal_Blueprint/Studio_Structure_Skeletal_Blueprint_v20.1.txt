## Architectural principle
`CC_AI_DESK` is **one persistent AI subsystem for the entire Classies Chronicles franchise**. A new AI Desk is never created for an individual episode. Episodes remain the canonical source of episode-specific truth; the AI Desk retrieves and references them when required.

The AI Desk therefore contains persistent intelligence, configuration, prompts, persona, relationships, tools, indexes and operational state — **not episode media or episode-specific personality state**.

The episode archive contains the durable source material. Retrieval indexes are disposable and rebuildable. This separation permits the archive to live on a local drive, Google Drive, another cloud folder, or a Git repository without changing the AI Desk's conceptual architecture.

The persistent AI Desk is responsible for:
- loading the canonical persona;
- maintaining approved relationship profiles;
- maintaining intellectual style, voice and behavioural rules;
- storing and versioning prompts and schemas;
- selecting an active episode;
- querying episode metadata and context;
- searching indexed transcripts and approved research;

It is **not** responsible for:
- being the canonical storage location for raw video;

- permanently copying every episode into its own memory;

# 3A. AI Desk Architectural Decisions

### Decision 11 — One persistent AI Desk
There is exactly one logical `CC_AI_DESK` for Classies Chronicles. Episode selection changes the retrieval scope; it does not instantiate a new AI Desk.

### Decision 12 — Episodes are the source of truth
Episode folders remain authoritative for episode-specific content. AI indexes, summaries and caches may be regenerated and must never become the only copy of source material.

### Decision 13 — Episode Context is mandatory
Every episode receives an `XX_EPISODE_CONTEXT` folder containing metadata, transcript, research, headlines, show notes, consent/licensing information and AI reproducibility information.

### Decision 14 — Persona evolves through governed diffs
The AI may propose personality or relationship changes after an episode, but the producer must approve the change before it becomes part of the canonical persona.

### Decision 15 — News is episode-scoped
The persistent Desk stores the news-curation method/prompt. Current headlines and episode-specific summaries belong to that episode's `XX_EPISODE_CONTEXT`.

### Decision 16 — Retrieval index is disposable
The index is a rebuildable cache. Losing it must not lose the Chronicles archive or persona.

### Decision 17 — Privacy and consent are retrieval gates
Member/guest material may enter retrieval only when the applicable consent and licensing state permits it. Revoked material must be removed from the retrieval corpus and future indexing runs.

### Decision 18 — Producer override is absolute
AI autonomy can increase within the defined modes, but AI MUTE and producer override remain independent of conversational autonomy.

# 9. Audio Gain Philosophy

The studio should solve weak audio primarily through:

1. microphone placement
2. microphone distance
3. appropriate source gain
4. room control
5. clean routing

rather than aggressive post-recording amplification.

A clean slightly quiet recording is recoverable.

A clipped or heavily distorted recording is not.

# 31. Evolving AI Personality

The AI personality belongs to the persistent `CC_AI_DESK`, not to an episode. Each episode may contribute observations that result in a **proposal** to evolve the personality.

## 31.1 Canonical Persona Files
```text
CC_AI_DESK/persona/
├── CC_AI_PERSONA_BASE_v0.1.txt
├── CC_AI_PERSONA_INTELLECTUAL_STYLE.txt
├── CC_AI_PERSONA_VOICE.txt
├── CC_AI_PERSONA_BEHAVIOURS.txt
├── CC_AI_PERSONA_BOUNDARIES.txt
├── CC_AI_PERSONA_RELATIONSHIP_{{buzzjuice_username}}.txt
├── proposals/
└── CHANGELOG.md
```

Relationship files are created only when a meaningful, approved relationship pattern exists with a host, guest or recurring participant.

## 31.2 Evolution Workflow
```text
COMPLETED EPISODE
      ↓
TRANSCRIPT / OBSERVATIONS
      ↓
AI PERSONA ANALYSIS
      ↓
SMALL DIFF PROPOSAL
      ↓
PRODUCER REVIEW
      ├── REJECT → ARCHIVE
      └── APPROVE
             ↓
       PERSONA VERSION UPDATE
             ↓
          CHANGELOG
```

Rules:
- no silent persona mutation;
- prefer small, reversible changes;
- maximum three base-persona changes per episode unless explicitly approved;
- relationship observations must identify the participant;
- conflicting observations remain proposals until resolved;
- historical persona versions remain recoverable through Git.

## 31.3 Persona Precedence
```text
BOUNDARIES / SAFETY
       ↓
BASE PERSONA
       ↓
INTELLECTUAL STYLE
       ↓
VOICE / BEHAVIOURS
       ↓
APPROVED RELATIONSHIP PROFILE
       ↓
EPISODE CONTEXT
       ↓
CURRENT USER / PRODUCER REQUEST
```

Episode context can shape the response but cannot permanently rewrite the persona.

`12_RETRIEVAL_REFERENCES.json` records the source identifiers used to answer significant episode queries. This prevents an AI response from becoming an untraceable statement.

The AI Desk reads or retrieves from this folder as required. It does not move episode context into the persistent persona directory.

```text
CLASSIES_CHRONICLES/
├── CC_AI_DESK/                 # persistent control plane
│   ├── persona/                # canonical evolving personality
│   ├── prompts/                # reusable prompt library
│   ├── schemas/                # data contracts
│   ├── scripts/                # ingestion/indexing/automation
│   ├── indices/                # rebuildable retrieval cache
│   ├── episodes_index/         # metadata catalog / pointers
│   ├── ui/                     # producer interface
│   ├── config/
│   ├── tests/
│   ├── logs/
│   ├── backups/
│   └── README.md
│
├── CC_S00_FOUNDATION_PHASE/
├── CC_S01_PLATONIUM/
├── CC_S02_.../
└── GLOBAL/                     # optional approved franchise-level research
```

## 57.1 Storage Authority
- Raw media: episode folders.
- Episode context: episode `XX_EPISODE_CONTEXT`.
- Persona: `CC_AI_DESK/persona`.
- Prompts/schemas: `CC_AI_DESK/prompts` and `schemas`.
- Episode catalog: `CC_AI_DESK/episodes_index`.
- Search embeddings/indexes: `CC_AI_DESK/indices`; disposable.
- Backups: separate physical/cloud destination.

## 57.2 Repository Options
The episode archive may be synchronized from:
1. local storage;
2. Google Drive/cloud storage;
3. Git repository;
4. a hybrid model.

The AI Desk should depend on **canonical paths/metadata contracts**, not on a specific storage provider.

## 57.3 Source-of-Truth Rule
If an index conflicts with an episode source file, the episode source file wins. If a cached summary conflicts with a current approved metadata file, the approved metadata wins.

# 57A. Indexing & Retrieval Architecture

The retrieval layer converts the episode archive into searchable context without making the index the source of truth.

```text
EPISODE ARCHIVE
      ↓
VALIDATE / DISCOVER
      ↓
METADATA CATALOG
      ↓
CHUNK TRANSCRIPTS / RESEARCH / HEADLINES
      ↓
EMBEDDING / SEARCH INDEX
      ↓
QUERY ROUTER
      ↓
SCOPE FILTER
      ↓
TOP RELEVANT SOURCES
      ↓
AI REASONING
```

### Retrieval scopes
- `ACTIVE_EPISODE` — only the selected episode;
- `CURRENT_SEASON` — all approved episodes in the active season;
- `ALL_APPROVED_EPISODES` — cross-season retrieval;
- `GLOBAL_CHRONICLES` — franchise-level canon and approved research.

### Retrieval requirements
Every result should carry:
- episode ID;
- source file;
- section/chunk;
- timestamp where available;
- consent/licensing state;
- retrieval/index version.

FAISS or another local index may be used initially. A hosted vector database may be introduced later without changing the source-of-truth architecture.

# 57D. AI Desk Prompt Library

### News curation prompt
The persistent prompt must request structured, verifiable results containing at minimum:
- title;
- one-sentence summary;
- category;
- location;
- publication date/time;
- source;
- URL;
- relevance to the episode;
- suggested conversational reference;
- verification status.

### Headline expansion
When the producer selects a headline, the AI may expand the selected item using fresh source retrieval and produce deeper context suitable for discussion. The expanded material remains episode context.

### Persona update prompt
The persona editor must output a minimal, reversible diff rather than rewrite the personality.

### Retrieval prompt
The retrieval prompt must instruct the model to distinguish:
- directly retrieved fact;
- reasonable inference;
- conversational suggestion;
- unsupported claim.

# 113. Version 20.1 Refactor Record

Version 20.1 incorporates the persistent-AI-Desk architecture and the feasible recommendations from the external GitHub review. The major changes are:

1. `CC_AI_DESK` is explicitly a single persistent subsystem rather than an episode-specific AI instance.
2. Episode folders are the source of truth for episode-specific information.
3. `XX_EPISODE_CONTEXT` is established as the mandatory episode/AI interface.
4. AI personality is persistent and versioned through governed diffs.
5. Relationship-specific persona files are supported.
6. Current news is stored per episode; news-curation methods remain persistent in the Desk.
7. Retrieval scopes and source provenance are explicitly defined.
8. Local FAISS/similar indexing is permitted as a rebuildable MVP; hosted vector databases remain optional.
9. A producer UI is defined without turning the UI into a second CMS.
10. Initial automation is decomposed into small scripts.
11. Episode metadata, consent and licensing are treated as retrieval gates.
12. AI reproducibility snapshots are required for completed episodes.
13. The commissioning sequence now builds the persistent Desk before live AI audio integration.
14. The architecture remains compatible with local folders, Google Drive/cloud folders and Git repositories.

## 113.1 Deliberately Deferred

The following remain optional and should not be introduced until the pilot demonstrates a need:
- hosted vector databases;
- network audio such as NDI;
- Voicemeeter/VB-Cable unless the physical audio topology requires it;
- autonomous multi-agent orchestration;
- complex cloud pipelines;
- automatic persona commits without producer approval.

The governing rule remains:

> **Build the smallest reliable system that can evolve into the larger system without architectural rework.**

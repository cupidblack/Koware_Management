# Lifestyle Cartographer

## Position purpose
Model lifestyle ecosystems, user journeys, environments and service relationships in evidence-based maps and specifications.

## Reporting and placement
**Department:** Lifestyle Research & Design  
**Reports to:** Enterprise Architect  
**Direct reports:** As shown immediately beneath the role in the source chart; otherwise none specified.

## Key duties
1. Research user needs ethically.
2. Map journeys, touchpoints and service dependencies.
3. Create service blueprints and design recommendations.
4. Validate models with users and teams.
5. Protect participant data and distinguish evidence from assumptions.

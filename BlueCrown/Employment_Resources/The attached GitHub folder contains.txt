The attached GitHub folder contains snaps of employment sheets that include a duty list and residential by-laws for a House-boy and Garden-boy.

The duty list should be combined into one duty list for a 'Residential Caretaker'. The duties should be spaced out and span on whole working week from Tuesday to Saturday, 6am to 6pm each day. The Residence bylaws should also be combined.

Thoroughly review and analyze the sapped documents of the duties and the bylaws then develop and update them appropriately to produce a new weekly duty list and residential bylaws for the caretaker.

Note the following:
<<START NOTES>>

1. Include one daily schedule that is generic for all days.

2. The daily schedule should have slots for day-specific tasks. For instance, on the generic daily schedule could be:

<<start>>
8.00-10.00: Day specific duties #1

2.00-4.00: Day specific duties #3

On the day-specific duty list could be:

Tuesday:
1. ....
2. ...

Wednesday:
1. ....
2. ....

<<stop>>

2. The caretaker being hired is 37 years old and only has senior high school qualifications. He is married with two children around or under 12 years of age. He would be staying on site at a room in the quarters. He would go home to his family on Saturdays and return on Mondays. He has agreed to a monthly salary of 1000 GHC per month.

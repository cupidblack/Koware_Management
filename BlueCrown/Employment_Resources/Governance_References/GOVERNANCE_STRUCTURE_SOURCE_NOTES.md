## Stakeholders — chart line 1

The chart lists stakeholder entities and contains the expression “God as CEO”. Preserve this as a statement of organisational values or faith-based framing, **not** as a registrable employment post, statutory directorship or evidence that named institutions have approved a partnership. Determine actual stakeholder rights, membership, representation, conflict management and communications through validated documents and their consent.

## Shareholders — chart line 2

The chart records a proposed shareholder list and percentages. The list is **not a verified statutory register**, proof of paid-up capital or current beneficial ownership, and no legal or voting rights can be derived from this explanatory note. Corporate Secretary and qualified advisers should reconcile the chart with authorised registers and instruments before any action or public claim. Treat the later Members Representative, Governing Committee and Board of Directors entries as separate structures with potentially different legal sources of authority.

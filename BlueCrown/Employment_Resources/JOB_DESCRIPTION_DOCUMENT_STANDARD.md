# Koware Group — Job Description Document Standard v0.4.0

**Document ID:** KOWARE-HR-JD-STANDARD · **Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Status:** DRAFT_AWAITING_HUMAN_REVIEW

## 1. Controlled architecture

Exactly one independently maintainable, substantive Markdown record exists for each of the **62 charted employment-position instances**. If a title is repeated in a future chart, each separately charted instance requires a distinct stable ID and folder. Use the existing `BlueCrown/Employment_Resources/Job_Descriptions/POSITION_SLUG/POSITION_SLUG_job_role.md` structure. Keep governance charters and ownership references separate. The doubled path segment appearing in the user example is an accidental repetition and is not a supported target.

## 2. Source and precedence

For job title, hierarchy and charted subordinates, use [the organisational chart](https://github.com/cupidblack/Koware_Management/blob/main/Organizational_Chart_Structure-Aliased_Checks.txt) on the approved branch, citing **1-based line numbers**. Indentation creates a proposed administrative reporting map, not a power of attorney. Anomalies or changed lines require chart-owner review and corresponding updates across the index, metadata and records. The original chart includes aliases, parenthetical notes, check days, and unbalanced delimiters in some alias text. Do **not** infer personal identity, qualifications, religious affiliation, appointment, work hours, pay, consent or formal legal authority from that text.

For powers and enforceable conditions, priority flows through actual applicable law and professional regulation, the legally effective constitution/bylaws and corporate resolutions, valid written delegations and approved policies/agreements. A chart or this draft cannot supersede any of them. Use legal review for conflicts.

## 3. Required content (every role)

Each document has YAML fields for ID, exact chart title, version, draft status, source chart and line, reports-to and directly reported roles. It must provide meaningful, **role-specific** purpose, at least six observable duties, three concrete deliverables, distinct authority/approval limits, proportionate qualifications, three performance indicators, risk controls, systems/records, handover and draft governance status. No empty `TODO`, `TBD`, `Lorem ipsum`, invented signatures or generic vacancy text is accepted.

## 4. Operational control rules

- **Decision versus activity:** staff can perform their tasks within an actual delegation, not self-approve high-risk decisions. Describe action, approver and escalation separately.
- **Audit independence:** the chart places finance and records below Administrative Auditor. Independent assurance must not review the auditor's own operating decisions. Arrange independent review/oversight as approved.
- **Academic assurance:** Quality Assurance & Compliance Manager may report administratively to academic leadership while preserving protected reporting of material findings.
- **Supervised interns:** an intern's charted subordinates require competent accountable adult oversight; do not grant the intern HR, finance, admission or legal decision rights.
- **Professional boundaries:** legal journalism is not legal practice, recovery coordination is not clinical care, technical roles do not grant engineering licences and domestic/grooming staff require safe, consent-based task scope.
- **Privacy, consent and fairness:** least-privilege records, respectful working conditions, accessible service, safeguarding and non-discrimination apply to all roles.
- **Check days:** recurring `Day N` text in the source is a project planning sequence; never reinterpret it as roster, hours, appointment or pay schedule.

## 5. Measurement, approval and revisions

KPIs specify observable measures without unapproved numerical targets. Targets must be calibrated with actual volume, baselines and resources, then authorised through normal performance processes. Compensation, hours, travel, grade, contract classification, location and effective date are defined by separate approved instruments. The complete collection stays a **draft** until verified against the source, department mandates, finance and delegation controls, HR practices, safeguarding, sector-specific licensing and Ghanaian legal advice. A signature or waiver is never fabricated.

Use semantic versioning for controlled document changes; material mandate or reporting edits require a documented review and may require a major version at formal adoption. Source-line corrections and migrations must update the master index and validation manifest together. Preserve historical work in Git commits, not as competing active descriptions.

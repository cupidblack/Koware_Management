# Koware Group — Job Description Document Standard

**Document ID:** KOWARE-HR-JD-STANDARD  
**Version:** 0.2.0  
**Status:** Draft — awaiting human review  
**Applies to:** Individual job descriptions under `BlueCrown/Employment_Resources/Job_Descriptions/`

## Purpose

This standard governs creation, review, approval and maintenance of role descriptions. It is not an employment contract, constitution, professional licence, policy or delegation-of-authority instrument.

## Required document architecture

Each position has one independently maintainable Markdown file and folder. Every role record must include a stable document ID; exact title and department; version and status; source chart, blob SHA and line number; source alias as unverified planning metadata where present; reporting parent and direct reports; position purpose; role-specific responsibilities; deliverables and records; authority and escalation; qualifications and competencies; proposed KPIs; conduct, privacy and safeguarding; health and safety; systems/resources; development; approval controls and revision history.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current approved organisational chart governs role title, reporting line and placement.
2. Constitution, bylaws and governance resolutions govern governance powers and accountability.
3. Approved policies and delegation instruments govern decision rights, expenditure, contracts, hiring, access and escalation.
4. Applicable law and professional regulation override inconsistent internal descriptions.
5. Platform responses, aliases and AI drafts are advisory only and are never evidence of appointment, qualification or authority.

## Drafting rules

- Describe the position, not a named individual.
- Preserve chart hierarchy; do not infer powers from senior-sounding titles.
- Distinguish accountability, routine discretion, approval rights and escalation duties.
- Use role-specific responsibilities, deliverables and indicators.
- Require qualifications proportionately; require and verify professional credentials only where law or assigned regulated duties demand them.
- Do not set numerical performance targets before workload, resources and baselines are confirmed.
- Do not invent salary, hours, benefits, location, employment status, statutory powers, financial limits or signing authority.
- Mark drafts clearly and use null for genuinely unapproved metadata.
- Record material revisions with date, version and concise change description.

## Approval workflow

Draft preparation → reporting-manager review → departmental review → Human & Employment Resources review → legal/specialist review where required → authorised governance approval → publication as approved version → periodic review.

Employee acknowledgement, if used, records receipt only unless a separate lawful agreement states otherwise.

## Versioning

Use three-part versions: major for mandate/structure changes, minor for substantive improvements and patch for editorial corrections. Drafts remain `DRAFT_AWAITING_HUMAN_REVIEW` until formal approval is recorded. Superseded versions must be retained or archived under the approved records-retention policy.

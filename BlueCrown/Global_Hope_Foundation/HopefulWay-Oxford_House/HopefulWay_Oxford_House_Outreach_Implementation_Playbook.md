# HopefulWay Oxford House Outreach & Resident Interest Programme

**Implementation playbook — prepared 26 November 2026**  
**Programme lead:** HopefulWay Foundation Chairperson  
**Primary first-phase objective:** Obtain voluntary, informed expressions of interest and move suitable applicants through a fair assessment for the currently operating Ashiaman men's house, while separately measuring demand for future houses.

## 1. Executive decision

Run two connected but distinct workstreams:

1. **Ashiaman occupancy and continuity (urgent):** confirm the house's current occupancy, actual vacant beds, financial terms, charter status, resident-approved admission process, and the lease decision date immediately. The supplied proposal says the house has two of eight places occupied, needs six residents by 31 December 2026 to continue, and its lease ends 31 January 2027. These are time-sensitive planning assumptions, not facts to publish until the Chairperson and house verify them. The deadline is close; make a continuation/closure decision with residents and the landlord rather than recruiting on a promise of availability that may not hold.
2. **Future demand and partnerships:** build consented interest by location and gender without representing proposed houses as open, chartered, or guaranteed. Do not mix future-demand registrations with current Ashiaman vacancies.

Do not begin mass outreach until the house facts, approved rules, privacy notice, secure form, response owner, and current presentation authorization are checked. Do not offer referral commissions in this pilot.

## 2. Scope and review of supplied materials

The supplied resources include the presentation authorization letter PDF, Oxford House videos and links, promotional literature, historical Oxford House administrative material and house-rule documents, and prior platform recommendations in `global_hope_foundation-responses.txt`. The prior recommendations that are adopted here are: focus first on the existing house; build a referral funnel; use a clear landing page and consent-based interest form; maintain separate institution and applicant registers; offer presentations; use planned, respectful follow-up; and distinguish interest, referral, assessment, house decision, and admission.

The folder also contains historical, US-specific records and old Ghana house-rule documents. They are reference materials, not proof of present Ghana operations, current charter status, current house availability, or current rules. In particular, do not circulate old US financial records, resident records, assessments, identity documents, or internal administrative files as promotional attachments. Select only current, authorized, public-facing material after a named owner checks it.

The approved/signed presentation authorization is not the same thing as an individualized application invitation or proof that a particular residence presently has a valid charter. Preserve the signed authorization unchanged. Make an institution-specific cover letter as a separate document only with approval; never paste recipient details into the signed master or imply a new approval/signature. If the signed final is not yet in the shared folder, upload it to the authorized internal location before outreach and restrict access as needed.

### Verification gate: Chairperson and house must sign off

Before advertising a place or saying “chartered,” confirm and record:

- Whether the Ashiaman residence currently holds an active Oxford House charter, its exact registered name, and the wording HopefulWay is authorized to use. A foundation's relationship or charter is not automatically proof that every individual residence is currently chartered.
- Current occupancy, actual vacancies, gender eligibility, location, accessibility, and whether the house is accepting applications now.
- Monthly resident contribution, deposits or other fees, utilities, payment timing, move-in costs, financial assistance policy, and what a person must be able to pay. Do not ask people to self-screen against a price until it is approved and communicated clearly.
- Current resident-approved rules and admissions process, including any substance screening, relapse response, emergency, safeguarding, complaint and appeal procedures. Old documents are not substitutes for the current rules.
- Current lease status, the decision deadline and whether the stated December/January dates remain accurate. Set a contingency plan if the house cannot continue.
- Who can receive applicant data, who can conduct screening, who communicates decisions, and the current Chairperson contact details.
- Whether presentation permission covers the proposed institutions, presenters, media, and use of Oxford House name/logo. Obtain permission for logos, testimonials, photos and video excerpts.

If a point is unverified, describe it as “to be confirmed” internally and omit the claim from external messages. Do not publish a blank, invented, or stale value.

## 3. Accurate, ethical positioning

Use this plain-language description: **Oxford House is a peer-run, democratically governed, self-supporting recovery residence. It is not a treatment centre, and a referral or interest form does not guarantee a bed.** Explain that the established model emphasizes resident self-government, shared responsibility and a substance-free living environment. Describe HopefulWay's Ghana implementation only to the extent current charter documents and house rules support it.

Avoid claims that a house prevents relapse, guarantees sobriety, is clinically supervised, or is inherently safer or better than every unlicensed/unchartered provider. Instead, explain verifiable governance and accountability features and invite institutions to inspect the approved charter and rules. Avoid disparaging or making legal claims about other providers.

Admission is voluntary and sequential: expression of interest → applicant contact and information → preliminary discussion → house application/interview → decision by the existing residents under current rules → written terms and move-in orientation. Never imply that a supervisor, sponsor, foundation executive, form submission, or preliminary interviewer can override the house's decision.

Any relapse, detoxification, testing, return-to-house, or eviction explanation must match the current signed house rules and charter. Do not promise automatic return after detox, or claim a failed/refused test always results in immediate eviction unless the currently applicable rule and due process have been confirmed. In an urgent medical situation, direct the person to appropriate emergency/clinical care; the house is not detox or treatment.

The supplied account of the Chairperson's personal recovery journey (about 13 years of addiction, repeated recovery attempts, three years in Oxford House, diploma completion, and ten years of sobriety) can be a powerful first-person testimony. Obtain the speaker's explicit approval for each use, preserve their wording, do not present it as a guaranteed outcome, and allow the speaker to omit any detail. A short version is included below.

## 4. Audience, priority and funnel

### Outreach priority

1. Greater Accra rehabilitation facilities: centre director, clinical supervisor, discharge planner, counsellor or social worker.
2. Addiction and mental-health services with discharge/referral roles.
3. Recovery organisations, NGOs, social-welfare and reintegration services.
4. Faith-based recovery programmes.
5. Educational bodies only where a counselling, student-welfare or re-entry contact can identify adults who independently seek recovery housing. Do not advertise this as ordinary student housing.

Start with a small, personally addressed pilot of 5–10 institutions, learn from responses, then expand. Use individual messages or an approved WhatsApp Business broadcast to contacts who have consented to receive such communications. Avoid exposing recipient phone numbers in a group chat and avoid unsolicited repeated bulk messaging.

### One shared status vocabulary

Track institutional relationships separately from applicant records. Applicant stages: `New interest`, `Consent/contact pending`, `Contacted`, `Information provided`, `Preliminary discussion booked`, `Preliminary discussion complete`, `House interview requested`, `House decision pending`, `Accepted`, `Not accepted`, `Deferred`, `Withdrawn`, `Closed/deleted`. A waiting-list entry is not an application approval or bed reservation. Record status changes and the responsible staff member, not clinical narratives.

## 5. Build the minimum safe infrastructure before outreach

### 5.1 Landing page

Use one public campaign URL as the canonical address: `https://buzzjuice.net/hopefulway-oxfordhouse`. The requested `https://koware.org/hopefulway-oxfordhouse` may redirect to it after the domain owner configures and tests the redirect. Neither link should be represented as operational until it has been published and tested on mobile, with the form working end to end. Page sections: current opportunity and status; short explanation of the peer-run model; confirmed Ashiaman facts; eligibility/house-process overview; costs and rules after verification; “what happens after you submit”; privacy notice; interest form; presentation request; official resources and video. Clearly label future-location/female interest as demand research only, not a current placement offer.

### 5.2 Applicant interest form

Form title: **HopefulWay Oxford House — Applicant Interest and Referral Registration**. Form introduction must say: “This form records voluntary interest so HopefulWay can contact you and explain possible next steps. It is not an admission application, does not reserve a place, and does not guarantee accommodation. The house makes its own admission decision under its current rules. You may ask questions or withdraw your consent.”

Prefer an applicant-completed form. A supervisor may help only with the applicant present or after the applicant has given informed permission. A sponsor's agreement does not replace an adult applicant's consent. The form must be available to self-referrals too.

**Collect only what is needed for first contact:**

1. Applicant's first name (required); surname (optional initially).
2. Confirmation that applicant is 18 or older (required; do not collect full date of birth at this stage).
3. Applicant's own phone/WhatsApp number (required if they want a call or WhatsApp); email (optional); preferred contact channel and safe/preferred time (required).
4. Current town/area (required; not street address).
5. Interest type: Ashiaman men's house, future men's house/location research, future women's house/location research, or first seeking information. Mark future options “not currently available; interest only.”
6. Preferred area as free text (optional) and willingness to consider Ashiaman (yes/no/need more information).
7. Current transition stage: in treatment, discharge planning, completed treatment, or another recovery/reintegration route. Do not collect diagnosis, substances used, detailed treatment history, medication, test results or clinical records.
8. Expected discharge / earliest possible move-in date, optional and only where relevant.
9. Ability to discuss and plan for the confirmed resident contribution: wants costs first / expects to contribute / needs to discuss possible support. Do not collect bank details or financial documents in this form.
10. Referral source category (self, rehab, health service, sponsor/family, recovery group, educational body, NGO/social services, other); referrer name and institution/contact only if the applicant agrees to their involvement.
11. Optional sponsor/support person name and contact, only if applicant authorizes HopefulWay to contact them; make clear this is optional.
12. Accessibility or communication support request, optional; do not request diagnosis.
13. Required applicant consent checkbox and privacy notice acknowledgement. Optional separate checkboxes for contacting the named supervisor and sponsor, and for future programme updates. No pre-ticked boxes.

Do not collect date/place of birth, emergency contact, gender identity, medication, diagnosis, sponsor contact, or supervisor details by default at first expression of interest. If a necessary admission-stage requirement later justifies additional information, explain why, collect it separately and securely, and obtain appropriate consent. Only ask gender where needed to route to a separate house; current house is described as male only after that is verified, and no co-ed accommodation should be implied.

**Consent wording for the form:** “I am voluntarily asking HopefulWay Foundation to contact me about Oxford House recovery housing. I have read the privacy notice and understand that submitting this form does not guarantee admission or accommodation. I can ask to correct or withdraw my information by contacting support@buzzjuice.net. I agree to the use of the contact details and information I have supplied for responding to this request and arranging next steps.” Add separate optional permissions: “I authorize HopefulWay to contact the supervisor/referrer I named about this enquiry” and “I authorize HopefulWay to contact the sponsor/support person I named about this enquiry.” The applicant can submit without either optional permission.

### 5.3 Privacy, security and retention

Recovery-related information can be sensitive personal data. Before collection, the Foundation should confirm its Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) obligations with its responsible privacy lead or Ghana counsel, including Data Protection Commission registration/compliance, lawful basis, notice, access/correction/withdrawal procedures, processor arrangements, retention and any cross-border/cloud processing. Do not launch until the privacy notice is published and reviewed.

Use a Foundation-controlled account; restrict the response sheet to named, necessary staff with multifactor authentication; do not make the sheet public; do not email full response contents to `support@buzzjuice.net`. Email only a submission alert with no sensitive details. Do not put applicant names or recovery status in public calendars, general WhatsApp groups, or the institution outreach register. No mass export/download to personal devices. Set and implement a documented deletion schedule for unresponsive/withdrawn enquiries and audit access. Tell applicants who will see their information, why, how long it is retained, and how to request correction or deletion.

### 5.4 Presentation booking

Use Amelia on Koware.org only after the owner has configured a public, tested appointment page, time zone, availability, confirmation/reminder settings, cancellation rules and recipient notifications. Keep booking data to institution, staff contact, preferred date/time, venue or virtual, estimated audience, accessibility needs and topic. Do not ask for named client lists. Notify the Chairperson and `support@buzzjuice.net` without sending applicant records. Until the booking page is live, supervisors can request dates by replying to the initial message or calling the Chairperson at the verified current campaign number. Do not invent a booking URL.

### 5.5 Separate registers

**Institution outreach register (non-client data):** institution ID/name/type/area; public contact and role; how contact was obtained and permission/status; assigned HopefulWay owner; first/last contact; response; presentation request/date; next follow-up; number of referrals only in aggregate; opt-out; notes limited to professional coordination.

**Applicant tracker (restricted):** internal applicant ID; submission date; consent flags; contact preference; interest location; transition stage; assigned staff member; stage/status; next action/date; deletion/withdrawal date. Store form responses separately from outreach register. Record no unnecessary clinical details.

## 7. Follow-up and nurturing rules

Follow up only with institutions for which there is a legitimate, appropriate contact basis and which have not opted out. Log every attempt and stop if asked. Use Day 0 initial contact; Day 3 brief receipt check; Day 7 one useful resource; Day 14 one call if appropriate; then pause. Recontact around 90 days only where the person has not opted out and a relevant update exists. Do not send daily reminders, pursue individual clients without their consent, or treat silence as permission for indefinite marketing.

For an applicant, acknowledge promptly; confirm their own consent and safe contact method; respond to questions; provide accurate rules, costs and availability before scheduling; offer the preliminary discussion; and update them if status changes. If no response, make no more than two discreet attempts through their chosen channel over approximately two weeks, then mark inactive and delete according to the retention policy. Never disclose their interest to a referrer or sponsor without the applicant's permission.

## 8. Applicant pathway and roles

1. **Expression of interest:** applicant submits, or explicitly agrees to a referral. Applicant gets acknowledgement and privacy information.
2. **Consent check and first contact:** designated coordinator checks consent and preference, confirms the person is at least 18, explains this is recovery housing rather than treatment, and asks whether they want to continue.
3. **Information conversation:** explain currently confirmed location/gender, actual availability, resident contribution, self-supporting/shared living expectations, rules, democratic decision process, interview steps and that acceptance is not guaranteed. Provide the current rules and cost sheet securely.
4. **Preliminary discussion:** schedule with applicant's agreement. The supervisor may help find a time only if the applicant agrees; the applicant should have a private opportunity to speak. Do not make clinical diagnoses or ask for unnecessary health details. Refer clinical or detox questions to qualified providers.
5. **House application/interview:** if the applicant wishes to proceed, share the approved application process and arrange the house interview with existing residents. Provide reasonable accessibility support.
6. **Residents' decision:** the house decides under its current charter/rules. Record decision and date; tell the applicant respectfully and privately. If deferred or declined, explain any available review/next steps consistent with rules and ask whether they want future-location updates.
7. **Admission and orientation:** only after acceptance, confirmed vacancy and agreed costs, provide written move-in arrangements, house rules, emergency and safeguarding contacts, and an orientation. Do not accept money before terms and authority are clear.
8. **Post-admission:** the house manages itself according to its rules; HopefulWay maintains only its authorized support/oversight role. Respect residents' privacy and democratic governance.

## 9. Presentation design and materials

Prepare a 15–20 minute accessible presentation plus Q&A:

1. The post-treatment transition challenge (without implying everyone relapses).
2. What Oxford House is and is not: peer-run recovery housing; not treatment, detox, or guaranteed accommodation.
3. Self-government, shared responsibilities, self-support and substance-free expectations, using only verified local rules.
4. HopefulWay's role and exact verified charter/residence status.
5. Personal testimony, with consent and no outcome promise.
6. Current Ashiaman opportunity and verified costs/availability/eligibility.
7. Referral/consent process and applicant privacy.
8. House interview and resident decision.
9. How to request a presentation or share the interest link.
10. Questions and a one-page contact/resource sheet.

The approved presentation letter can be shared with the initial outreach only if the signed version is available and the Foundation approves it for distribution. It is reasonable to attach it to the first personalized institutional message, alongside one concise current one-page factsheet; avoid a large bundle. Do not attach internal admin files or ask institutions to forward the letter as an admission guarantee. For institution-specific letters, create a separately approved cover letter addressed to the actual recipient and institution, with the institution's real address and current date. Preserve the signed authorization unchanged.

## 10. Compensation, expansion and housing viability

Do not pay supervisors, sponsors, staff at treatment facilities, or referrers per applicant, per admission, or as a percentage of rent in this campaign. Such payments create conflict-of-interest, safeguarding, procurement and trust risks, especially where people are vulnerable. If a future institutional service agreement is considered, obtain independent legal/ethics review, written Foundation approval, transparent terms, no influence over clinical discharge or house admission, and disclosure to applicants. A non-financial partnership, staff education, and transparent expense reimbursement are safer pilot options.

Do not promise a female house or a house in another town based on a waiting list alone. Assess sustained voluntary demand, safe separate premises, charter/organizational approval, resident leadership, local legal requirements, staffing/support boundaries and a viable budget first. Do not combine genders in accommodation while the programme does not support co-ed houses.

Create a weekly Ashiaman viability dashboard for the Chairperson and residents: verified occupancy/capacity; voluntary qualified interest; interviews booked/completed; offers/declines; monthly income and costs at aggregate level; lease decision; and safe contingency options. The stated target of six residents by 31 December must be treated as an internal management target only, not used to pressure applicants. Make no move-in commitment without actual bed availability and a viable lease.

## 11. Step-by-step implementation schedule

### Days 1–2: decision and verification

- Chairperson convenes house residents and Foundation decision-makers.
- Verify charter and the exact authorized public description; house rules; costs; vacancy; occupancy; lease facts/deadlines; current contact and signatory; and who owns each action.
- Decide whether Ashiaman is actively accepting referrals now and what to tell applicants if the house closes or changes.
- Approve the consent, privacy, safeguarding, relapse/emergency and complaints process. Obtain Ghana privacy/legal review as needed.

### Days 2–5: minimum viable system

- Foundation privacy lead approves the notice, retention schedule, access list and response workflow.
- Build the minimal Google Form using Section 5.2, under the Foundation-controlled account; configure notification-only email to support@buzzjuice.net and lock the response sheet.
- Publish the form on the canonical landing page with current facts, video, official links and acknowledgement. Set up a redirect from the second domain only if its owner can configure it.
- Configure Amelia; if not ready, publish no false booking link and use reply/call scheduling.
- Create separate institution and restricted applicant registers; assign a named owner and backup for every new enquiry.
- Test from a phone as an applicant and as a supervisor; test acknowledgements, consent fields, access, email notification, deletion request, calendar timezone, and link redirects. Remove test entries securely.

### Days 5–7: staff readiness and pilot

- Review approved signed letter, current public brochure/factsheet, house rules, cost sheet, and official model language. Remove stale or unverified claims.
- Rehearse the 15–20 minute presentation and the questions about cost, testing, relapse, treatment, gender, privacy, house voting and availability.
- Compile and verify 5–10 Greater Accra institutions and the correct professional contact. Assign one owner to each and record source/permission.
- Send personalized pilot messages; record response, referral permission and next action. Do not use a group broadcast exposing numbers.

### Weeks 2–4: refine and scale

- Apply the Day 3/7/14 sequence only where appropriate; pause on opt-out or decline.
- Hold presentations by appointment; offer the interest form to individuals, not as a requirement for attendance.
- Review weekly funnel measures and applicant experience with the Chairperson and house representatives. Fix confusing form questions and page friction before expanding to additional institutions.
- Reconfirm Ashiaman lease and capacity weekly until its future is settled. If it cannot continue, contact every active applicant privately and offer only verified alternatives or an opt-in future-interest record.

### Monthly: governance and learning

- Review aggregate measures, privacy incidents/requests, complaints, opt-outs, follow-up completion, resident feedback, house viability and expansion demand.
- Delete expired records; review access; retrain staff; update the public factsheet and form when facts change.

## 12. Measurement and launch criteria

Measure: verified institutions approached; delivery/response rate; presentations requested/booked/completed; voluntary applicants; percentage with applicant consent confirmed; contact time; preliminary discussions; house interviews; resident decisions; admissions; withdrawals; opt-outs; and demand by location/gender in aggregate. Track the conversion funnel without setting quotas for vulnerable clients or rewarding referrers.

**Do not launch until all are true:** current residence status and rules verified; truthful cost/availability information available; signed authorization handled correctly; privacy notice and lawful collection process approved; restricted form and registers tested; Chairperson and backup assigned; response/withdrawal process tested; and messages approved by the Foundation and house representatives.
